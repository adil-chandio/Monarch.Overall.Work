# Mafia character sheet

- `mafia-full-body-character-sheet.png` is the assembled front, three-quarter, profile and back character sheet.
- `mafia-hair-sheet.png` is the earlier face and hair reference sheet.
- `body-front-identity.png`, `body-three-quarter-identity.png` and `body-profile-identity.png` reuse portions of the supplied face references as scaled, blended composites over AI-generated body views. The three-quarter body was mirrored to match the supplied face angle.
- `body-back.png` is the AI-generated back view; `side-revised.png` and `back-revised.png` are the hair-reference edits used for the sheet.

The suit, shoes and full-body proportions are design interpretations, not measurements from the reference. Compositing involves resizing and blending, so the resulting pixels are not identical to the originals and some transitions may remain visible.
