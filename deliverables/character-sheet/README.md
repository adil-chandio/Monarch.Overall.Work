# Mafia character sheet

- `mafia-full-body-character-sheet.png` is the assembled front, three-quarter, profile and back character sheet.
- `mafia-hair-sheet.png` is the earlier face and hair reference sheet.
- `body-front-identity.png`, `body-three-quarter-identity.png` and `body-profile-identity.png` reuse portions of the supplied face references as scaled, blended composites over AI-generated body views. The three-quarter body was mirrored to match the supplied face angle.
- `body-back.png` is the AI-generated back view; `side-revised.png` and `back-revised.png` are the hair-reference edits used for the sheet.

The suit, shoes and full-body proportions are design interpretations, not measurements from the reference. Compositing involves resizing and blending, so the resulting pixels are not identical to the originals and some transitions may remain visible.

## AI full-body revision

- `mafia-full-body-ai-v2.png` pairs the supplied face references with newly AI-generated full-body views; it replaces the earlier composite for review.
- `ai-full-front-v2.png`, `ai-full-three-quarter-v2.png`, `ai-full-profile-v2.png`, and `ai-full-back-v2.png` are the individual AI-generated views.
- These views are generated from the references, not exact pixel-preserving face composites. Facial likeness and body proportions can vary; the source images do not establish actual full-body measurements.

## 3D draft download

- `character-reference-draft.glb` is a static, approximate face-textured 3D prototype, not an exact scan or finished realistic character. It contains no skeleton, animation, physics or cloth simulation.
- `character-reference-draft.zip` packages the same GLB with a brief note for easier download.
- **Privacy:** The GLB embeds the user's reference image. This repository is public and so are these files. Uploaded at the user's request.
