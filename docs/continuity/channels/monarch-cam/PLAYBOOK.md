## 2. Two series

### Primary — `Too Close: Wildlife in Human Space`

Each episode answers one behavioural question through real footage:

> **What happened → what the camera actually proves → why the animal did it → what happened next → which related story should viewers watch next?**

This is a mini-documentary format, not a random clip dump.

### Secondary — `Nature's Hidden Tricks`

Use only after the primary cluster is established: camouflage, visual puzzles, unusual adaptations, “can you spot it?” discovery. Do **not** run funny pets, instant karma, trains, aviation, horror and ocean fear between these uploads.

## 3. Viral-quality chain

```text
High-demand concept
  → instantly readable real thumbnail
  → accurate standalone title
  → proof in the first 3 seconds
  → payoff by 0:25
  → fresh evidence/explanation throughout
  → satisfying final answer
  → highly related next video
```

Hashtags cannot compensate for a weak link.

## 6. Shorts — discovery, not main revenue plan

Two Shorts per long video:

- **Short A:** strongest action/proof.
- **Short B:** one behavioural insight or consequence.

Each Short stands alone but makes the long episode the obvious next destination. Use YouTube Studio **Related Video** where available; first description line `Full story: [long-video URL]`; pinned comment; final-second cue `Full story on Monarch Cam`.

Short rules: animal/action readable on first frame; one moment, not a five-clip countdown; no unsupported `3 AM`, location or species detail; no rights-unverified footage; no random pet/karma upload between series Shorts.

## 9. Rights and originality gate

Owner preference recorded 5 November 2026: **no direct permission-request outreach workflow**. Default footage pool:

1. Public-domain footage with asset-specific declaration.
2. Creative Commons/stock footage whose actual licence permits commercial use and intended edits.
3. Original narration, diagrams, maps, typography, animation and safely filmed demonstration material.

Do not treat crop, mirror, zoom, speed changes, filters, credit, disclaimer, `Section 107` or fair-use paragraph as copyright clearance. Editing alone does not clear arbitrary copyrighted sources; YouTube reused-content review and copyright enforcement are separate.

First bear episode: use verified NPS public-domain source pack in `MONARCH_CAM_PERMISSION_FREE_BEAR_FOOTAGE_PLAN_2026-11.md`. CPW clip is factual research only; its CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence restricts commercial use and modification.

Every new episode needs a clip ledger before edit: `Clip ID`, `Original URL`, `Rights basis`, `Usage evidence`, `Date verified`, `Credit`, `Editorial addition`, `Backup`. Each long video must add original narration, a clear editorial question, sourced behavioural context, substantive story structure, meaningful visual explanation and a distinct Monarch Cam point of view.

AI may assist with outlines, research organisation, non-realistic diagrams, captions and production. It must not fabricate a wildlife event and present it as real evidence. Realistic altered/generated material requires disclosure where applicable.
