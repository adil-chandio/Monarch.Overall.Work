## Conclusion

The previous batch's `claude-real-video` and `reelbench-skills` remain more relevant to frame and shot analysis **once a video is available**. Navigator could complement them for transcript search, but captions do not establish what appears on screen. The new network-level repository addresses a different failure mode from our evidence; the two viewers are designed for view inflation. None of these five demonstrates access to or frame-by-frame inspection of our blocked competitor videos. The Google official-video API service remains disabled in the tested project; activation requires authorized project administration.

Probe code: `scripts/yt_navigator_transcript_probe.py`; workflow: `.github/workflows/yt-navigator-transcript-probe.yml`. It stores only outcome and caption segment counts, not video/audio or transcript text. Until an Action actually runs, results are unknown.
