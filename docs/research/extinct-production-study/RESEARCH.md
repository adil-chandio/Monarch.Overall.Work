## Production-practitioner sources read

1. **Kurzgesagt — official production process:** https://kurzgesagt.org/what-we-do?visit=videos . Their own account describes research, repeated script development, bespoke visual metaphors, voice-led animation timing, mixed 2D/3D methods and specifically composed music/SFX. We adopt the workflow logic—not their distinctive characters or an assertion that our current pipeline equals their studio craftsmanship.
2. **Storybench interview with Vox art director Joey Sendaydiego:** https://www.storybench.org/how-vox-uses-animation-to-make-complicated-topics-digestible-for-everyone/ . The practitioner describes developing story-specific art direction, a recurring visual anchor, a clock-to-pie-chart metaphor and responsibility for accurate maps/stakes. We reject the page's unsupported visual-learning statistic and do not turn a quoted retention average into a target/guarantee.
3. **Google Earth interview with Vox producer Sam Ellis:** https://medium.com/google-earth/how-vox-video-uses-earth-studio-for-dynamic-visual-storytelling-703fc871766e . Actual spatial context and project locations can carry explanation instead of a list. We adopt purposeful map scale/detail—not decorative camera flying.
4. **Vox Borders — primary workflow account:** https://www.vox.com/a/borders/how-we-made-this . Story selection used strong visual evidence and context, backed by substantial reporting and collaboration. We adopt evidence-first selection; we do not claim possession of their field footage or production resources.

These sources explain intended practice. They do NOT prove all graphics use 12 fps, all sound hits match a beat, music changes every 20 seconds, or that some arbitrary shot count causes virality. Those promotional/generalised claims are excluded.

## Conclusions actually warranted

- References visibly alternate **evidence forms and explanatory tasks**, not just camera angles. This directly addresses V4's main creative failure.
- Effective graphics can identify place, compare anatomy/relationships, explain time/process and mark uncertainty. They are not obliged to animate every word.
- Some source editions include repeated illustrations, long host shots and simple treatments. They are not flawless templates; our owner's feedback remains decisive for this film.
- Anatomy, credible material/weight, asset rights and actual sound quality remain production work, not problems solved by JSON labels or a successful decode.
- **Exact source SFX/music/easing forensics remain unverified.** Our proposed soundtrack, text timing and transition durations are design recommendations, not invented observations.
