# What changes in our original script workflow

This is the implementation consequence of the expanded script reading, not a promise of virality. The canonical bilingual v2 script remains unchanged during this research pass. These changes are a concrete edit plan for its next revision, **not a claim that a new master, fresh narration, or final film already exists**. No new owner approval gate is introduced; the approved 3D direction remains authorized. Rendering stays on hold until the evidence/script/timing work is ready.

## 1. Use the correct story engine

The Greenland film is an **evidence investigation**, not a survival list or monster tournament.

- M01/M03: borrow a stable audience question and anticipated objections. Reject invented survival times and inevitable attacks.
- M02: borrow a physical setup with non-identical outcomes. Reject treating a small sample as a universal calibrated experiment.
- M04/M05/M07: borrow rule clarity, category variation, and an earned final perspective shift. Reject size-only escalation and arbitrary matchup rules.
- M06: borrow a recurring anchor, causal transitions, and an opening-object return. Reject false precision about reconstructed events.
- P03/P08/P11: borrow evidence that changes an initial model. Reject presenting association, inference, and direct observation as the same thing.
- P09/P10: borrow ordinary material becoming a window into a hidden world. Reject metaphors that secretly imply whole genomes or literal resurrection.

**Original organizing question:** How much of a vanished community can scientists recover from dirt when almost everything once alive is gone?

The animal makes the question vivid. The sample carries the investigation. The assemblage is the larger reveal. The limit of reconstruction makes the ending honest.

| 7. These are scene functions, not new scientific claims. Any new physical/mechanistic detail must enter the science ledger before it enters the master. Do not silently replace the English master with only a punchier owner-review story.

## 3. Add a manual beat ledger before another voice session

Each actual sentence/beat gets:

`beat_id | master_block | audience_knows_before | question_or_objection | evidence/action | changed_belief_after | payoff_or_bridge | science_claim_ids | uncertainty_near_claim | English/Urdu_pair | intended_visual_subject`

An example of **original planning language**, not competitor text or final VO:

- Before: the viewer imagines a buried skeleton.
- Objection: how can we claim an animal from dirt alone?
- Evidence/action: distinguish damaged mixed fragments from a complete recovered organism; show why comparison and checks matter.
- After: the viewer understands that the reconstruction is an inference from molecular traces.
- Payoff: the surprising material is already identified; the remaining question is how reliable and ecologically informative it can be.
- Bridge: the very fragility of the clue makes preservation the next necessary problem.

Do not create a universal quota such as a hook every five seconds. A beat must change knowledge, available options, stakes, confidence, or emotional interpretation. A cut alone does none of these.

## 4. Track open questions and repay them

Maintain a small explicit loop ledger:

| Loop | Open when | Close with | May remain open? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Why mastodon in northern Greenland? | First block | Community/environment interpretation plus independent context | Exact individual circumstances remain unknown. |
| How could DNA last this long? | When fragment fragility matters | Preservation conditions and extraction explanation | Detailed molecular history is not completely reconstructed. |
| How can it be identified? | Candidate fragments appear | Reference comparison, damage/contamination checks, uncertainty | Species/lineage resolution must remain as supported by the paper. |
| What did the place look like? | Several ecological clues accumulate | Qualified reconstruction using the assemblage | Exact positions, interactions, and simultaneous co-occurrence are not established. |
| What has actually been recovered? | Central question | Explicit final answer: partial information about an ancient community | No intact genome/resurrection promise is allowed to remain dangling. |

The aim is to resolve earlier questions while introducing necessary next ones. Keeping every mystery open until the ending is not the strategy observed in the strongest cases.

## 5. Give metaphors a falsification check

For each analogy, ask: **What false physical thing might the viewer now believe?**

- Archive/library metaphors can imply intact complete books. Pair them with fragments, mixed sources, and missing pages only if those images remain accurate to the actual method.
- Puzzle metaphors can imply every piece belongs to one predetermined full picture. Mixed environmental DNA is not that simple.
- A time machine can imply a literal simultaneous scene. Label the reconstruction and explain its scope.
- A protective surface can imply guaranteed survival. Preserve the dependence on conditions and damage.

P09's explicit rejection of a motorized tooth whorl is the useful lesson: repair the false image, do not trust the audience to infer the caveat.

## 6. Keep entertaining certainty separate from scientific confidence

For every sourced statement classify:

1. **Observation:** what was actually measured/found.
2. **Supported inference:** what comparisons and controls support.
3. **Model/estimate:** what depends on assumptions or extrapolation.
4. **Reconstruction:** a labeled depiction consistent with evidence but not an observed event.
5. **Unknown:** what cannot currently be concluded.

The modern scripts demonstrate why this matters: spectacular metaphors, hypothetical fights, exact local scenes, and model maxima often migrate into authoritative-sounding facts. Public popularity is not scientific validation.

## 7. Revise English and owner-review Urdu together

- Revise the canonical paired JSON, not two independent documents.
- Retain the same claims, caveats, reveal order, and ending in both languages. Preserve two million years as بیس لاکھ, not a mistranslated number.
- Check scientific claim IDs and reference resolution after every substantive change.
- Run the existing script-lab tests and generate the human-readable files from the same canonical source.
- Generate **fresh** natural narration after the text stabilizes. Measure both languages; the English 6–8-minute target is not proof the translated review track fits the same duration.
- No stretching, silently dropping caveats, or reusing the rejected full-film narration to disguise a new master.

## 8. Bridge script to the approved moving 3D film

The new research contains no verified competitor shot log. It cannot justify claims about their exact cuts, motion, music, or sound design.

For our film, independently enforce the existing requirements: correct subject/action, meaningful coherent world motion, purposeful ≤3-second shots, spatial/action continuity, premium audio, and actual 1080p delivery. A sentence may span several shots, but the shots must advance one understandable action. Do not use a shot counter as a proxy for storytelling quality.

Before rendering: semantic beat review → science-ledger review → faithful bilingual review → measured fresh VO → phrase-based animatic → continuity/subject check → full production in the already approved direction. This is an execution workflow, not another request for style approval. No YouTube upload is authorized.

## 9. Tests we can and cannot pass now

**Available now:** corpus provenance, coverage honesty, API snapshot identity, cue counts, source-order coding, original-analysis presence, render-hold state, canonical script integrity.

**Still not available:** causal retention effects, Tier-1 audience composition, CTR, growth rate from one snapshot, fresh-v3 duration, cold-listener comprehension, final motion quality, final soundtrack, or a completed film. Do not put a check mark on any of those because a Markdown packet or unit test passed.
