## Active deeper pass

`scripts/stickman-curated-windows.py` / `.github/workflows/stickman-curated-windows.yml` now requests **24 complete one-second bursts across 12 human-selected references**. It verifies each re-acquired movie's SHA against the previously indexed source, exports **every decoded frame inside each exact source interval**, and measures short-window audio RMS without claiming listening. The intervals were chosen after actual image inspection to investigate eye/attention transitions, symbol→reaction, prop handling, physical assistance, chase posing and spatial transitions. This is **reference extraction**, not production of the unapproved experimental minute.
