## Blocking evidence

The initial public playback probe failed. A second attempt installed the supported Node JavaScript runtime and yt-dlp's official playback components, and tested eight relevant videos rather than unrelated top search hits. **0/8 full videos were acquired**: YouTube returned “Sign in to confirm you’re not a bot.” No cookies, private credentials, authentication bypass, proxy evasion or DRM circumvention were used or requested.

Creator-site/alternate-platform web searches did not establish an accessible equivalent broad corpus of the same eligible, million-view, rendered-3D videos. General 2D Vimeo results cannot silently replace the requested 3D cohort or carry over YouTube view counts without matching evidence.

Consequently: **zero videos have been frame-by-frame visually studied in this research phase.** Search snippets, statistics, descriptions and thumbnails are not substitutes for the actual footage. No completed forensics report or production approval request is justified yet.
