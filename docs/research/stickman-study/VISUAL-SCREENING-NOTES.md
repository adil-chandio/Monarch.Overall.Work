# Actual-footage screening and initial craft findings

**7 November 2026 — research in progress, not production approval or a completed exhaustive study.**

The access problem is no longer “no watchable footage.” Public archival movies have now been acquired, fully mechanically decoded and their actual image sequences opened for visual screening. V9 is unchanged; no new one-minute experimental animation has been produced.

## 1. What was actually inspected

Bounded sharded run **42382841043** recovered **27 of 31 selected video files** and indexed **326,594 decoded video frames**, with actual decoded presentation timestamps. All 27 generated overview sheets were opened and examined: **24 sampled panels per file, 648 overview panels**. Six selected primary-source micro-window sheets were also opened: **60 panels**, including consecutive central frame triples and surrounding sampled frames.

A separate small-file control pass **42382617679** recovered four complete clips, **3,138 decoded frames**. Their four overview sheets and four micro-window sheets were inspected: **136 panels**, plus one rescaled detail quotation. Combined mechanical processing is **329,732 decoded frames**; that number is **not** a claim that every frame was consciously visually interpreted.

**Important coverage limits:**

- Overview labels are requested seek times, approximately within a decoded frame; exact native-stream PTS is in the compressed frame indices and micro-window captions.
- Existing five-panel micro-windows use offsets −3, −1, 0, +1, +3. The central three are consecutive; the whole five-panel strip is not a complete unbroken five-frame run.
- “Detail” JPEGs are rescaled to 960 pixels wide. They do not establish native source resolution or restore missing detail.
- No complete uninterrupted audiovisual viewing, heard soundtrack assessment, per-joint rig reconstruction, original authoring-software proof, retention measurement or audience-geography verification is claimed.
- The strongest automatically selected changes often land on flashes, hard cuts or credits. Those are useful for edit analysis but are **not automatically useful contact/weight windows**. The next deep pass must use manually selected acting/action intervals, listed below.

## 4. Four contrast clips: useful failures, not viral success evidence

**Rafehi Studios, Stickman 3D — `149101adaa69`, 46.68 s, 1,167 decoded frames.** The sampled course, blocks, stairs, checkered gates, orbiting perspective and shadows provide a visibly three-dimensional obstacle-course scene. The character is extremely small and low-contrast relative to the textured grass and pale course, including the inspected 33.12 s detail. Many panels mainly change the view of the course, not the readability of the actor's intention. Blue title/end text competes with that already small subject. **Learning:** genuine dimensional rendering does not automatically yield a premium animation; protagonist scale, silhouette, readable contact and purposeful camera decisions still matter. No million-view claim is attached.

**Epic stick figure battle, parts 1/2/3 — `ffb57ce30e33`, `1b40e0d1dba4`, `c4596dafd64f`.** Flat red/black characters on white, FlipaClip watermark, exaggerated size hierarchy, reaction/question marks, smears and title/subscribe cards. These are clearly **2D low/unverified-reach contrasts**, not replacements for the primary 3D target. Part 1's question-mark close-up at 7.33/8.03 s corresponds to 21 indexed frames in that interval with almost no reduced-resolution change. Conversely, part 3's similar-looking chin/hand overview samples at 21.94–24.97 s hide substantial intervening changes: 73 indexed frames, maximum 64×36 mean difference 37.78819, only 45 exactly zero-change frames. **Do not call that entire interval a static hold from three matching thumbnails.** This is direct evidence of the danger of sparse sampling.

## 5. What is now evidence-backed — and what is not

### Initial transferable findings

1. **Intention and response make motion matter.** Opposed eye/pose states and visible reactions are stronger evidence of acting than camera motion alone. Basement Busk's attention/match transition is a concrete example.
2. **Persistent props are narrative anchors.** Devices, cups, fan/weapon, staff, bow and geometry retain identities across framing changes. Recolouring or replacing a prop is not equivalent to showing its interaction.
3. **A returning location need not be a repeated scene.** Changed objectives, constraints, actor relationships and accumulated environmental consequences distinguish a return from a duplicate setup.
4. **Diagram/text can be part of the mechanism.** Math/Geometry and the original interface film place symbols where the actor can act on them. This is different from long top captions or decorative labels.
5. **3D alone is not the quality lever.** The Rafehi control has depth but weak actor readability. Conversely, dimensional shading in a stronger 2D film is not proof of a 3D mesh pipeline.
6. **Constant visual intensity is not the goal.** The inspected science examples contain high-brightness flashes and dense notation; these should not be copied indiscriminately into the desired smooth, comfortable science film.
7. **Numerical frame coverage is not interpretation.** Change peaks preferentially find flashes/credits; uniform sampling can both miss motion and exaggerate apparent repetition. The manual action-window pass is necessary.

### Still not justified

- “We fully watched/interpreted every frame of 27 animations.”
- “We studied 27 viral pure-3D stickman films.”
- “Their views prove these visual choices caused virality, or prove US/UK/Canada/Australia reach.”
- “Their rigging, contact physics, foley comfort or sound/music alignment has been comprehensively established.”
- A new production approval request based on pretending those gaps are closed.
