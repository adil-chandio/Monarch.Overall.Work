# Scientific and creative evidence ledger

## Primary research

Kjær et al. (2022), **A 2-million-year-old ecosystem in Greenland uncovered by environmental DNA**, *Nature*. DOI **10.1038/s41586-022-05453-y**. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05453-y

Supporting explanation: Natural History Museum, December 2022. https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/news/2022/december/worlds-oldest-dna-represents-two-million-year-old-ecosystem.html

These are the sources reviewed during this project's earlier research, carried forward here. This film is not a new 2026 discovery report, a new independent laboratory verification, or a claim about the current oldest-DNA record.

| Section | Supported core | Limits applied on screen / in narration |
|---|---|---|
| Opening / The place | Kap København, northern Greenland, roughly two-million-year deposits; vegetation evidence, including birch, poplar and conifers | Approximate age and locator; concept coast, not study-site footage; older pollen/plant evidence acknowledged |
| The invisible trail | Environmental DNA can include material from different organisms in sediments | General explanatory model; transport/time integration prevents a single-afternoon interpretation |
| The mineral shelter | Cold conditions and mineral interactions are relevant to unusual persistence; binding may help protection | No immortal DNA, exact decay rate, sample-specific atomistic model or actual molecular microscopy claimed |
| Reading the pieces | Sequences compared with reference genomes; identification depends on reference coverage and fragment information | Bars, letters, library rows and branch structure are illustrative, not actual reads, matches, success rates or a measured phylogenetic tree |
| A community reappears | Reported signals associated with plants, hares, reindeer, geese and a mastodon lineage, among other organisms | Not every identification has equal precision; no exact coat/size/behaviour recovered; no literal simultaneous community scene |
| The missing photograph | Genetic evidence is not an observed interaction or a complete inventory | Non-detection is not proof of absence; artist-supplied appearance and arrangement are distinguished from evidence |
| Different windows | Plant remains/pollen predated DNA findings; different archives provide complementary information; age is geological/approximate | No invented specimen counts, numerical confidence values, exact birthday or literal calibrated timeline |
| What the ground kept | Fragments permit partial inference of a vanished community | No intact genome, cloning, resurrection, frozen living animal or current-record claim |

The mechanism animations explain concepts; they do not reenact a documented laboratory procedure or actual depositional event. Species anatomy, locomotion, plants, coast, lighting and ecological arrangement are stylised artistic interpretations, **not validated reconstructions**. The mastodon is a lineage stand-in, not a species-accurate portrait inferred from DNA. The film does not demonstrate a migration route.

## Map / rights

Greenland polygon: Natural Earth 1:110m countries, public domain. https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Coarse outline; approximate Kap København point around 82.4°N, 22.4°W. Not a survey or high-resolution site map.

All other visual scene content is original procedural vector/raster drawing and animation code, derived from the approved Amber Atlas opening and extended with new plant, pollen, sediment core, mineral, laboratory, comparison, animal and evidence-integration systems. No stock scene plate, AI-generated image, acquired photograph or competitor screenshot is used.

Narration is synthetic English using the user's previously selected session voice `voice-00`. Eight new clips are preserved as lossless FLAC; their decoded PCM hashes are in `voice-provenance.json`. Score, synthetic air and schematic material cues are original mathematical synthesis. No authentic extinct-animal calls, Greenland field recordings or commercial score are claimed.
