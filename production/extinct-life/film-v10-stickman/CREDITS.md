## Original visuals and voice

Original procedural Three.js scene, articulated amber investigator, teal scarf, handled props, snow/sediment diorama, illustrative DNA fragments, trees, puzzle and conceptual elephant-like animal. No generated image assets, reference footage, copied reference characters or choreography. Native animation rendered at every output frame, not still-image camera pans. Camera and object trajectories are authored stylization, not physically validated simulation.

Uses the first two original approved English voice-02 assets at their existing chapter start times, without speed changes: B01 and the first 7⅔ seconds of B02. Ends after the complete sentence “Living things shed biological material.” Subtitles retain the original wording and alignment. The 61-second duration avoids cutting “material”; this is not a retimed narration or replacement story.

## Science and illustration limits

Kjær et al., *Nature* (2022), “A 2-million-year-old ecosystem in Greenland uncovered by environmental DNA.” https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05453-y

The published interpretation concerns ancient sediment and environmental DNA; the fragments are enlarged explanatory symbols, not recovered whole genomes or exact molecular geometry. The elephant-like model and growing vegetation illustrate an inferred past, not a recovered portrait, exact appearance, exact co-occurrence or photoreal reconstructed field footage. Living shedding illustrates that death is not required to leave biological traces. No claim of a current oldest-DNA record.
