## Character, worlds and animation

New original procedural Three.js research room, exposed sediment bank, microscopic explanatory set, polar landscape, conceptual past vegetation and woodland/stream. Camera moves through spaces as an observer; hands, sleeves and tools carry actions. A brief opening over-shoulder view establishes the stylized investigator before predominantly first-person staging. This is not a continuously visible external character model standing on a presentation platform.

The uploaded portrait sheet is **not redistributed** in the release or committed source. The procedural stylized character is not an exact likeness, a photoreal human, or a claim of anatomical validation.

Original procedural mastodon geometry from the retained V9 source is reused as a 3D mesh and animated in the new scenes; **no previous movie frames are reused**. Geometry source: `production/extinct-life/film-v9-full/native-meshes/mastodon.glb`; original production sculpt, not a recovered portrait or reference-video character. Simplified gait/deformation is illustrative, not a physically validated simulation.

Two pre-existing production clay/silt material maps are applied to rendered 3D surfaces: `production/extinct-life/flow-proof/materials/clay-albedo.jpg` and `clay-height.jpg`. The original albedo was AI-generated as a material texture; its grayscale derivative is not measured displacement. They are **not scene plates, panned pictures or new image generations**. Other cloth/wood/grain textures are deterministic procedural textures. No reference animation footage, choreography, characters or soundtrack copied.

## Science and interpretation

Kjær et al., *Nature* (2022), “A 2-million-year-old ecosystem in Greenland uncovered by environmental DNA.” https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05453-y

Fieldwork, room, research handling and wildlife scenes are explanatory staging, not documentary footage of the original researchers, laboratory protocol, exact site or an observed ancient ecosystem. Enlarged DNA fragments are explanatory symbols, not visible-to-the-eye DNA, complete genomes or exact molecular geometry. Animal appearance, habitat and co-occurrence are illustrative/inferred. The book is an original evidence prop, not a reproduced journal page. Shedding does not require death; the leaf/animal staging illustrates living biological traces, not proof that any particular sampled fragment came from the depicted individual. No claim of a current oldest-DNA record.
