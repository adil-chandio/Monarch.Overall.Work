## What is deliberately NOT marked complete

1. Rigged, anatomy-reviewed living-world animation with convincing contact, weight and continuous character action.
2. Full-film rough animatic covering all 58 visual tasks. This excerpt demonstrates only nine process/graphic tasks; it does not satisfy the entire mixed-technique proof gate by itself.
3. Final music/stem and foley selection/rights, precise ear-approved synchronization and listen-through.
4. A resolved coherent final visual finish across photographic-style concepts, painterly community imagery and authored graphics. The style frames are choices to evaluate, not proof that final cohesion is solved.
5. Whole-film creative review, native final render, final QC and final delivery.

**Do not replace these remaining tasks with slowly panned still images or declare a completed premium film because the exporter passed.** No new YouTube upload, voice audition or paid purchase was made.
