## 1. Recommended direction: cinematic evidence documentary + editorial motion graphics

Ek coherent hybrid style:

1. **Living-world moments:** carefully staged 3D/illustrative reconstructions, used where an animal/environment actually advances the story. Not an elephant walking across most of the film.
2. **Evidence/process moments:** distinct macro material, transport cross-sections, sediment sections, sample handling, sequence alignment and reference diagrams. Har mechanism ko apni visual language aur action mile.
3. **Editorial layers:** place labels, short kinetic statements, a source/date card, traced routes, selective highlights and explanatory callouts. Yeh narration ke meaning ko clarify karenge; generic dashboard UI nahi.
4. **Meaningful sound:** room/landscape continuity, physical foley and an evolving score. Har visual entry par pop/whoosh nahi.

Amber / mineral ivory / deep teal palette aur consistent typography brand ko ek rakhenge. Maps, paper, water, specimens, molecular diagrams aur living habitats ki composition/material/action alag hogi. Hum Kurzgesagt ke characters, Vox ke layouts ya kisi aur ki PNGs/music copy nahi karenge.

**Realism boundary:** realistic staging, weight, contact, light and surroundings target hain. Ancient scene footage, exact mastodon coat/behaviour ya recovered complete genome ka claim nahi. Existing toy-like animal meshes automatically premium-approved assets nahi ban jaate; anatomy, rig, contact and material quality ko separately pass karna hoga.

## 3. Repeat ko rokne ka real rule

Hum ab “18 seeds / 58 IDs / new camera angle” ko novelty ka proof nahi maanenge.

Har proposed beat ko yeh pass karna hoga:

- **Naya explanatory task:** viewer ab kya naya samajhta hai?
- **Substantial new staging/state:** object arrangement, environment/process or result materially badla hai?
- **Purposeful visible action:** kuch meaningful hua—ya sirf camera orbit/fragment spin chal raha hai?

Only zoom, light, recolour, caption or random seed badalna **fail**. Same full composition/action track ko filler ki tarah dobara use karna **fail**. Same scientific subject zaroor laut sakta hai, lekin naye question, new composition and progressed state ke saath. Damaged/lost DNA agle shot mein magically heal nahi hogi.

**Examples:**
- Mastodon opening = trace/contradiction; community chapter = short lineage profile; reconstruction chapter = distant staged behaviour with explicit uncertainty. Same walking shot in three forms nahi.
- DNA hook = what the evidence is; mineral section = why some may persist; comparison section = how identity is assessed; ending = what remains incomplete. Same floating helix, different zoom nahi.
- River first use = delivery; later reminder = space/time limitation, through a new explanatory composition—not old transport footage again.

## 4. Text, PNG/SVG elements aur motion ka exact role

### Text
- One dominant short statement at a time, usually 1–6 words; longer terms handled as separate readable labels.
- 1080p starting targets: main statements roughly 48–84 px, callouts 30–40 px, scientific qualifiers/citations at a readable size. Final mobile-size review decides, not a font-size checklist alone.
- Words arrive on the relevant spoken emphasis, not random beats. On-screen negation must remain intact: **NO SIGNAL ≠ NO ANIMAL** cannot momentarily read as the opposite claim.
- No full paragraphs over animated backgrounds; optional English subtitles remain a separate accessibility track.

### Elements
- Original transparent cutouts/vector plates: reviewed lineage portraits, botanical silhouettes, specimen annotations, grain sections, reference strips, route arrows and restrained source cards.
- PNG is a delivery format, not an aesthetic. Low-resolution stickers with white edges, mismatched shadows and random emoji/confetti are excluded.
- Max one primary hero + a small explanatory layer + necessary label at a time. More layers must earn their place.
- No fake study charts, invented sequence reads/percentages, fabricated paper screenshots or unsupported animal counts.

### Motion / transitions
- New idea develops through reveal → action → consequence → handoff.
- Restrained eased entry; readable hold; clean motivated exit. Initial entry ranges around 0.3–0.6 s are design starting points, not mandatory timing formulas.
- Object/shape continuity, screen direction, water/grain/leaf transfers, restrained focus and audio bridges carry the viewer. No universal blur preset between every subject.
- No split-video panels, rapid random camera changes, repeated entrance presets or overactive bouncing type.
- If a visual stays for longer, the subject/action/information must genuinely develop. An unchanged hero/action for roughly 8–10 s triggers a review flag—not an automatic cut.

## 5. Music aur SFX production plan

**Current procedural score ko sirf louder karke premium declare nahi karenge.** Music quality/rights must be resolved before a full film render.

- Select/clear an appropriate composed instrumental score or stems; original commissioned music is another option if budget permits. No purchase without approval, no ripped competitor score.
- A free-cleared option needs its exact track licence checked. “Royalty-free” search text alone is not permission.
- Story arc: curiosity → transport momentum → fragile survival/tension → precise investigation → community reveal → caution/space → reflective payoff.
- Music continues across visual handoffs where appropriate. Not a fresh 8-second loop or music reset on every sentence.
- Foley groups: ground/weight, leaf/wood, flowing/settling water, fine grains, paper/specimen handling, one liquid delivery, restrained read/reference movement, one wing/takeoff action.
- Actual contact, reveal or conceptual accent motivates a sound. Every label does not need a ping; every transition does not need a whoosh.
- Smooth attack/release dialogue ducking; intelligibility on phone and headphones; stereo image must not pull attention away from the words.
- Starting technical target: roughly −16 LUFS integrated, with conservative final AAC true-peak headroom. Measure the encoded export, not just the WAV. The v4 limiter lesson remains: no automatic makeup surprise and no weakening the test to get a green tick.

**Listening approval is separate from numerical QC.** A waveform or loudness report cannot certify that the score is moving, the foley is convincing, or that a video sounds like a reference.

## 6. Workflow after approval — not another blind full render

1. **Lock the scene board:** narration and all 58 visual tasks, source/uncertainty notes, asset ownership and reuse ledger.
2. **Resolve quality-critical assets:** animal anatomy/rig/materials; genuinely distinct environments; original graphic system; cleared music and foley. Missing premium assets cannot be disguised by smooth camera movement.
3. **Make six representative style frames:** living world, Arctic locator, mineral macro, reference comparison, community gallery, closing record. These are approval-stage originals, not frames stolen from references.
4. **Make a short mixed-technique animatic/proof**, including text, physical contact, one diagram-to-world handoff and actual mixed sound. It must demonstrate variety, not only one easy mineral scene.
5. **Review the full rough animatic against the narration** before expensive native rendering: no narrative gaps, no filler repeat, no distracting graphics, no unsupported visual claim.
6. **Only then animate/render the complete film**, finish audio, inspect contacts/edges/text/word timing, watch through the whole export, measure final encoded output and publish the versioned download.

No “perfect / hypnotic / guaranteed retention” promise. Better visual reasoning, originality, intelligibility and finish can be reviewed; virality cannot be guaranteed.
