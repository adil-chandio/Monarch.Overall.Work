# Implementation and acceptance specification — proposed, not production authorization

## What changes technically

The previous render/assembly infrastructure may provide frame scheduling, deterministic rendering and encoded-output QC. **Its few scene recipes are not the new creative plan.** Do not merely point the v4 camera at the same worlds and composite titles over them.

- **Living / material scenes:** original or cleared reviewed models, rigged where needed, in a scene-based 3D renderer. The repository's Three.js path can host GLTF rigs, purposeful animation tracks, contact/occlusion/shadow and environment staging. Blender is an alternative authoring tool if appropriate assets require it—not an installed/approved capability being asserted here.
- **Editorial graphics:** authored vector/SVG/Canvas layers, timed to the existing narration in a deterministic browser/compositor path. Use actual sequence alignment, route drawing, evidence masks, label tracking and controlled typography—not a universal pan/zoom on a PNG.
- **Integration:** render shared spatial subjects and graphic anchors consistently; composite at native 1920×1080/30 fps with safe text bounds, colour and alpha-edge review. Avoid “beautiful 3D beneath unrelated UI.”
- **Finishing:** cleared music/foley stems, deliberate contact/action cues, dialogue-led automation, final AAC measurement and complete-frame decode. Do not publish an unlistened mix as “perfectly matched.”

This is a proposed implementation route. Asset discovery/rig quality, actual music choices and a reviewed mixed-technique proof remain unresolved production work; a schedule/JSON recipe does not establish them.

## Asset packages and dependency gates

| Package | Required content | Acceptance / provenance gate |
|---|---|---|
| HERO-ANIMAL | Reviewed mastodon-lineage model/rig or an anatomically coherent illustrated treatment with genuine limited pose/action capacity | Correct lineage rather than a mammoth/modern-elephant substitution; believable joints, trunk, feet/contact and mass; no claim of exact recovered appearance; original/cleared licence recorded |
| COMMUNITY | Distinct hare, reindeer, goose, rodent and plant-group illustrations/rigs at a coherent finish | Names match approved script; scale obvious; a gallery does not imply a literal photographed encounter; no unreviewed AI anatomy |
| LIVING-SETS | New opening edge/trace staging and a substantially distinct imagined river margin; plants/ground/water composed for their actions | No old forest MP4; a recoloured forest or changed camera is not a second set; no generic standing/moving animal loop to fill unrelated narration |
| PRESENT-LOCATION | Distinct modern polar-desert establishing composition plus a different closing gravel/record task | Illustrative, not fabricated expedition evidence; correct broad geography and present/past distinction |
| TRACE-PROCESSES | Distinct shedding, decay, stream cross-section, mixed-source route and layered-deposit diagrams | Explicit schematic scale and transport uncertainty; age belongs to deposits, not direct molecular dating; no made-up historic catchment |
| MATERIAL-MACROS | Designed breakage, unreadable information, cold/qualitative loss, mineral binding, protection and release compositions | State changes persist; no lost fragment returns intact; no whole genome floating through every stage; no invented measured rates |
| LAB/READOUT | Extraction/sample handling and distinct contamination-control task | Illustrative workflow, no invented equipment claim; never a microscope “reading” DNA letters; no physically endless/repeating pipette delivery |
| IDENTIFICATION | Reference strips, ambiguity/branches, reference gaps, alternative explanations and damage/check diagrams | Schematic sequences marked as such; no fake source reads, plots, confidence percentages or independent damage-is-proof claim |
| SOURCES/PROXIES | Typeset source reference, visible plant-remains/pollen treatments, supplementary-evidence windows | Citation accurate; specimen/reconstruction status clear; original illustrations or specifically cleared source imagery |
| UNCERTAINTY/FINALE | Reconstruction qualifiers, unknown field-guide details, absence logic, complementary gaps and partial-record closing motif | Never turn incomplete record into clone/complete inventory; never erase all knowledge just because some is uncertain |
| GRAPHIC-SYSTEM | Typography, arrows, labels, clear source card and boundary styles | Consistent line weights, material/shadow language and readable mobile scale; no sticker edges/random fonts |
| SOUND | Rights-cleared coherent music/stems and motivated physical/detail effects | Exact source/licence/attribution/cost recorded; actual ear review required; source reference soundtracks not reusable assets |

**Licence ledger before import:** asset ID; creator; original URL; download date; exact licence/version or written grant; attribution obligation; allowed commercial/derivative use; source file hash; modifications; cost/approval; use in which composition IDs. Public availability or another creator's “fair use” disclaimer is not a licence for our film.

No paid asset/music purchase without owner approval. No competitor image, extracted PNG, sound effect or music cue is carried from the research sheets into our film. Repository research quotations remain separate from production assets.

## Review gates

### G0 — current decision
Owner approves/revises the direction and all-film structure. **No new film render yet.** Existing approved script/voice remain locked.

### G1 — asset/style package
Six representative style frames and the licence/anatomy/graphic-system checks. The earlier 24 s 3D proof remains historically approved; this is not reopening it. The new gate evaluates **new visual variety, typography, integrated graphics and sound direction** that the old proof did not establish.

### G2 — mixed-technique proof and full rough animatic
Show materially different visual modes with an actual sound mix, then the whole narration covered at rough quality. Owner/production review should reject:
- Consecutive variants of one forest/grain/lab setup masquerading as variety.
- Whole classes of narration covered by the same orbit/walk/spin/pipette action.
- Text that repeats obvious words without helping an inference.
- Nonsmooth arrivals, split-video clutter, mismatched graphic layers, inappropriate effects.
- Unsupported exact reconstruction, source precision, ages, sequence data, counts or confidence.
- Repeated short music loop, distracting effects, unclear voice or numerical loudness “approval” without listening.

### G3 — full production / technical and creative review
- All 58 planned tasks have a new-setup/action/result review entry, not just an ID. If a composition fails creatively, revise it; don't add more nominal beats.
- Full narration, all chapter boundaries and 13,163 native frames covered. No silent script rewrite, revoice, speed change, intro padding or recut old full film.
- Contact/shadow/rig/anatomy, text anti-aliasing/edges/legibility, transition continuity and scientific meaning checked at intended output size.
- Native full export decoded with no failures; actual AAC loudness/true peak measured; SRT timing checked.
- Human full visual watch-through **and listening** must be explicitly recorded as performed or remain an unmet gate. Never infer them from scripts, contact sheets, ffmpeg success or a green Actions run.
- Final delivery only after creative review: versioned MP4/SRT/QC/provenance on GitHub. No YouTube upload.

## Open risks, not hidden promises

1. Realistic animal anatomy/rig/material quality may require a cleared external asset or a deliberately illustrative style. Generic procedural geometry alone has not met this owner's premium expectation.
2. Premium score/foley selection needs a lawful source and actual listening approval. We have not heard competitor mixes through the available inspection route.
3. Estimated word alignment does not automatically make text and effects frame-perfect; final timing is a review task.
4. 58 planned compositions are a coverage proposal. Neither quantity, 30 fps, native resolution nor determinism proves a film is interesting.
5. The accessible study is sampled visual evidence plus text/process research—not a complete microscopic AV audit of every viral source. The limitation must remain visible when claiming research-backed decisions.
