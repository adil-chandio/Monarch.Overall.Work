## What is being built here

A **60.97-second selected 3D development excerpt**, not the finished 7:19 film. Three evolving spatial stages cover representative transport, time-averaged deposition and the explicitly imagined living-world encounter. Original approved voice-02 excerpts are used without speed change or a rewritten script.

- `worlds.mjs`: new 3D transport and exposed-deposit stages; living-world stage extends V4's actual mesh environment, lights/material style and infrastructure. No old film MP4 is recut.
- `animals.mjs`: V3/V4 lineage stand-ins are retained as the style baseline, with stance-phase foot locking, added knee continuity, reduced ear proportions and an articulated goose neck/head/foot flight pose. These remain stylized illustrative assets, NOT expert-validated animals or recovered appearances.
- `motion.mjs`: absolute-time root/foot/flight trajectories. With a planted stance foot, root translation is compensated rather than dragging the foot along the ground. Takeoff occurs once; the bird progresses away rather than returning to its starting spot.
- The native scene adds branch displacement linked to animal passage, transient water rings on takeoff, visible downstream movement and successive depositional arrivals. These are illustrative animation mechanisms, not calibrated biomechanics or molecular/transport simulations.
- Camera motion follows subject development. Tags are anchored to projected world locations; there are no left-hand presentation slides or full-screen still-image scenes.
- Native frames are individually rendered at **1920×1080 / 30 fps**, not generated by interpolating screenshots.

## Images vs textures

No V5 scenic PNG, AI scene plate or competitor footage is used. This does **not** mean meshes are forbidden to have textures: the original approved proof's clay texture and procedural bark/ground materials are used as 3D surface materials. The clay bump is illustrative, not a measured height map.

## Sound — a material change from the earlier tone-loop approach

The intended score candidate is **“Hiraeth” by Scott Buckley**, downloaded from the composer's official public link, with permission and attribution requirements checked against the primary track page. `MUSIC-LICENSE.json` records the source and CC-BY 4.0 requirements. A failed/blocked download is an error—not permission to quietly substitute the rejected procedural score.

If this is ever uploaded to YouTube, the required credit must also appear in the description. No YouTube upload is being made.

Physical water/contact/wing/grain details remain original synthesized sound design, **not recorded foley**. Actual listening approval is still required; loudness checks cannot certify taste, emotional fit or precise perceived synchronization.

## Checks

- `node motion.test.mjs`: stance-foot world-space locking, boundary continuity, nonnegative lift and exactly one forward-progressing takeoff. These are mechanical checks, not expert anatomical validation.
- `render.mjs --stills`: representative native scene frames, deterministic out-of-order seeking and a frozen-camera comparison that must still contain subject motion. A moving camera alone is not accepted as animation.
- Six non-overlapping render ranges cover the excerpt; final assembly requires every range, no gap/overlap and no recorded browser exceptions.
- Final encoded AAC loudness/peak and complete native frame decode are measured; credit and music-download hash are preserved in `QC.json`.

## Creative/inspection limits

Native representative frames and deterministic state changes can be inspected here. No full human continuous watch/listen or professional biological review is claimed. Earlier local visual checks prompted corrections to knee gaps, ear scale, reference-stage geometry winding/lighting and the depositional cap seam.

This remains stylized V4-based CGI. **Neither its 3D implementation nor its technical tests establish “perfect/cinema/top-notch” quality.** The whole-film variety problem is not declared solved by three excerpt stages. Full 58-task implementation, stronger asset/material finish, the complete animatic and full-film creative review remain unfinished. Do not present this selected excerpt as the completed film or silently fall back to image slides.

## Reproduction

```sh
npm install --no-save --package-lock=false three@0.187.0 playwright@1.64.1 @sparticuz/chromium@153.0.0
python -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install imageio-ffmpeg==0.8.1 numpy==2.4.6
python production/extinct-life/film-v6-motion/prepare.py
node production/extinct-life/film-v6-motion/motion.test.mjs
node production/extinct-life/film-v6-motion/action.test.mjs
node production/extinct-life/film-v6-motion/render.mjs --stills
# Ranges must partition schedule.json total_frames. Render and then finish:
node production/extinct-life/film-v6-motion/render.mjs --from=0 --to=1829
.venv/bin/python production/extinct-life/film-v6-motion/finish.py
```

Normal public composer access is needed for the music download; no cookies/DRM/geo/auth bypass. `finish.py --voice-only` provides narration-only live-review audio when offline; it is explicitly not the finished scored mix. Generated media stays outside Git in `.cache/extinct-v6/`, with release delivery through the tracked Actions workflow.
