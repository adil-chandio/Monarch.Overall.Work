This saber-toothed cub is over 35,000 years old. And its fur is still here. Found in Siberian permafrost, these are preserved remains, not a living animal. Scientists studied its skull and teeth using CT imaging. The animal was Homotherium, an extinct saber-toothed cat. Its tooth development suggests it was only about three weeks old. Compared with a similar-aged lion cub, it had smaller ears, a powerful neck, and broader front paws. Those soft-tissue details are things a bare skeleton cannot show. But the mummy is incomplete. A full living appearance still needs reconstruction. Not a resurrected predator. A tiny survivor of deep time, revealing what bones alone leave out.
