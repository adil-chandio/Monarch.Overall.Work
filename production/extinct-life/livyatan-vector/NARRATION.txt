Meet Livyatan. A sperm whale. And the only predator we know of that could have looked a Megalodon in the eye. It lived about nine million years ago, in the same oceans, at the same time. And its head held something Megalodon did not have. Teeth. Each one forty centimetres long. In both jaws. Megalodon had rows of teeth. Livyatan had four dozen spears. And it is not the largest whale that ever lived. It was about seventeen metres. A blue whale is more than twice that. So the question is not how big was it. The question is, what was it hunting? Megalodon teeth have been found with damage that matches something very large biting back. But the evidence is not complete. No one has found the two animals together, in the act. So we cannot say they fought. We can only say that for a few million years, two giants shared the same water, and one of them had teeth like this. That is not a monster. That is an animal that was really there.
