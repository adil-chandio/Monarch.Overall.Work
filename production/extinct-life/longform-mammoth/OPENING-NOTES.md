# 3D opening — production notes

**Status: work in progress, not a completed or approved film.** The corrected narration, visual sources and tests are in `opening-3d/`. The prior local MP4 is obsolete: it predates both science and visual corrections. Do not publish or deliver it as the current cut.

The new voice take replaces an overconfident opening: the 2017 study predicted a satin-like coat from FOXQ1 mutations; it did not directly observe loss of insulation. The 2024 study analysed additional ancient genomes and inferred effective population recovery after a bottleneck; it was not a finding that the same single genome proved a recovering population. The on-screen schematic curve is labelled as an effective-population model, not a genetic-diversity curve. The final generations are missing from the 2024 genetic sample, not necessarily from all evidence. See `opening-3d/SOURCES.md`.

Visual testing continues. The current wool remains too faceted and patchy for the premium-quality target. A real 30-frame encode test of the denser candidate took ~3.27 seconds per frame on two CPU cores with software rendering; a cheaper candidate took ~2.17 seconds per frame but still needs visual work. The production-path 1.5 seconds/frame target has not yet been reached. These are test measurements, not estimates of a normal GPU renderer.

The voice/caption alignment is approximate and needs listening review; the 60-second film has not been rendered with this corrected take. The old long-form `SCRIPT-V1.md` remains a draft with language that requires the same scientific correction before further production.

No YouTube publication is authorised or performed.
