# 3D opening — production notes

The 60-second opening is rendered at `deliverables/Last-Mammoths-Opening-01-3D-1080p.mp4`. The original procedural Three.js scene, corrected narration, audio design and production files are in `opening-3d/`. This is a stylised reconstruction, not a photoreal or archaeological scan.

The narration distinguishes a predicted 2017 coat phenotype from direct observation, and attributes the effective-population recovery inference to the later genomic study. The on-screen curve is a schematic model of effective population size, not a headcount or a diversity curve. The 2024 genetic sample lacks the final ten generations; this does not mean that all other evidence is absent. Sources are in `opening-3d/SOURCES.md`.

The English captions are aligned to the actual unsped voice take using PocketSphinx and the known transcript, then offset to the narration start. Automatic alignment is an estimate; no continuous listening review is claimed. The film has been fully decoded and its loudness measured, with one-second still sampling; `opening-3d/DELIVERY-QC.json` records the measurements. Passing those checks does not certify creative quality.

The full long-form `SCRIPT-V1.md` remains a research draft and needs a complete science review before further production.

No YouTube publication has been authorised or performed.
