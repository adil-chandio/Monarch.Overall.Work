## Creative idea: “Ek nishaan, poori duniya”

Film ka hero rotating DNA ya case-board nahi: **ek mammoth aur uski duniya ka nishaan**. A living reconstructed animal leaves a footprint; the world changes over thousands of years; a bone becomes evidence. Har evidence clue humein kisi meaningful action, living landscape, ya nayi question par le jata hai. Hum repeatedly poochte hain: **“Yeh clue kya bata sakta hai—aur kya nahi?”**

- **Emotional hook:** Ek living herd aur uski vulnerability; kisi specific mammoth ki unobserved death invent nahi karni.
- **Intellectual hook:** Same island ke evidence mein apparent contradiction: recovered population, lasting genetic strain, aur unexplained ending. Recovery ka model aur kisi individual ke genes alag cheezen hain.
- **Visual hook:** Scale changes—snow at foot level → herd and island → evidence under researcher’s hand → the scale of an inherited change. Macro visuals tabhi aayenge jab woh ek question ko solve karein.
- **Promise:** Audience ko ek honest verdict aur clear uncertainty milegi, fabricated killer nahi. Retention ki guarantee nahi; actual audience testing zaroori hai.

## Edit grammar: smooth, lekin monotonous nahi

1. **Motivated shot changes:** Generally 3–6 seconds mein naya viewpoint, action ya visual information; yeh rule nahi ki har shot force se cut ho. Complex thought ko readable hone ka waqt. Long shot tabhi hold jab animal, camera, atmosphere aur story visibly develop karein.
2. **Transitions plan:** Direction/action match (moving trunk → researcher’s brush), foreground wipe (animal/body/rock crosses lens), focus pull, light/colour bridge, aur audio J-cuts/L-cuts. Inke beech deliberate clean cuts bhi ho sakte hain. Har transition dissolve nahi: morph/whip/shake repeat ya disguise ke liye nahi.
3. **Continuity:** Scene change par camera direction, movement, framing aur sound carry ho. Spatially unrelated places ko impossible continuous real-time journey nahi dikhayenge. Time-compression, reconstruction aur hypothesis clearly label honge.
4. **No filler DNA:** Helix as wallpaper nahi. Genetics mein macro change → living consequence uncertain → scientist/sample/model. Molecular imagery conceptual, sequence/location fabricated na lage. Graphics limited, legible, evidence-driven; graphs actual source data se verified hon, 3D image-slide ya giant card nahi.
5. **Natural performance:** Mammoths ke liye researched elephant-based references, weight, staggered footfalls, stance contact, trunk reach, body inertia, gait changes, fur aur snow interaction. Living-elephant behaviour direct mammoth observation nahi. No artificial suffering, dead animal ya invented final-day event.
6. **Sound:** New restrained musical score, unhurried breath/air, snow/footfalls aur relevant lab sounds, smooth bridges. No helicopter/factory pulse ya recycled rejected drone. Voice always clear; no random whooshes per cut. Human listening and phone-speaker checks required.
7. **Safety of meaning:** Physical samples, locations and researcher activity are reconstructions, not photos of real evidence. A made-up animated event is not a finding. Captions checked for accuracy, safe margins and mobile readability.

## Sound and transition map

- **Opening → island:** quiet organic air and grounded footsteps; a warm/cool orchestral texture enters under the question, not rhythmic engine-like throb.
- **Island → lab → coat:** textured environmental sound narrows to intimate brush/glove/handling; one motif carried by tonal texture rather than loud swoosh at each edit. No invented direct recording claimed.
- **Environment/humans:** rain and snow respond to the action; archival/survey handling is restrained. Hypothetical sequence receives a distinct restrained tone, not horror shorthand for a proven disaster.
- **2024 reversal:** harmonic lift timed to modelled recovery, tempered by the returning strain motif. Narration and numbers have priority over music.
- **Coverage/missing:** subtle space and quieter score; no mechanical pulsing. End resolves without announcing a killer.
- Every source of music/SFX must be licensed or originally created; mix and final AAC loudness/true peak are measured, but **human headphone and phone-speaker listening** is mandatory before quality claims.

## Production prerequisites and approval gates

1. **Script editorial:** Compare full `SCRIPT-V1.md` with the adaptation; make a detail-by-detail disposition, keep supported details or justify omissions, correct overclaims, tighten repeated coat/microbe paragraphs and get final wording approved before retakes. If full original wording is demanded, flag the scientific conflicts instead of silently restoring them.
2. **Asset/art:** Obtain properly licensed, anatomically credible rigged woolly mammoth and distinct environments, or build and review them. Current procedural mammoth is a crude kinematic prototype; the lab and molecular recut are stylized and do not meet the requested standard. A rig licence and provenance must be verified; no unlicensed conversion of an elephant asset passed off as a mammoth.
3. **Animation:** Reviewed limb/trunk/body rig, plausible skin/fur movement, terrain contact and snow response. Elephant reference informs but does not prove extinct-animal behaviour. Establish continuity and natural performance before full rendering.
4. **Science review:** Verify data, paper figures, terminology, specimen ages and dates. Reconstructions and hypothetical scenes clearly marked; predictions and causes not presented as observation. Obtain expert review where possible.
5. **Edit/audio QC:** Listen to all takes, confirm exact word timings and captions, build/clear original score and SFX, inspect transitions frame by frame, test at full size and on phone, and check technical encode. Audience retention and subjective excellence cannot be certified by automated tests.
6. **Feasibility:** Existing 720p software tests required minutes for 30 seconds; this full film at the proposed asset and animation quality needs substantially more production/rendering capacity and asset work. Do not claim it can already be produced to feature-film standards with the current crude rigs. Full 3D, no image-backed slides.
7. **Review gate:** Approve creative direction and corrected script approach first. After approval, production still requires the asset, animation, sound, render and review steps above. GitHub delivery is intended; YouTube publication is not authorised.
