# Chemistry section — the second witness (305–354 s)

The narration calls chemistry "the second witness" and then immediately narrows what
that witness can testify to. This 49-second section follows that order: what each
isotope system can report on, what the 2019 comparison found, and then the caveat that
does most of the work.

## Structure

| Shot | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0–16.5 | Four systems — carbon, nitrogen, sulfur, strontium — each card stating **what it reports on** and nothing more: `diet`, `diet and trophic position`, `water and bedrock weathering`, `local geology and water`. Then: every value comes from a particular animal, tissue and span of time |
| 1 | 16.5–34.5 | `2019 · WRANGEL COMPARED WITH OTHER NORTHERN REGIONS`. Carbon and nitrogen each drawn as a **band labelled broadly stable**, then "That weighs against some gradual dietary changes", then the limit: it does not prove the herd was healthy or that conditions were easy |
| 2 | 34.5–49.0 | `NOT A WEATHER STATION`. Eleven sample points over a wide unrecorded field; a stable sampled signal can coexist with seasonal stress, and a rapid event can escape a record sparse in time |

## What is deliberately absent

- **No isotope values, no axes, no plotted series.** The narration says "broadly
  stable"; inventing a curve with numbers on it would be fabrication dressed as data.
  The band is the honest form of that sentence.
- **No weather imagery.** The narration's own metaphor — "not a weather station
  reporting every storm" — is quoted in the shot title and then left as a diagram.
  Drawing a storm would assert a season the record cannot show.
- **No animal shown eating.** The plates stay printed-paper; the earlier procedural
  animal studies were rejected as toy-like and are not reused here.
- **No claim that stability equals health.** The section states the opposite in the
  narration's own words and gives it equal visual weight.

## Limits

- Eleven sample marks are a **visual quantity**, not the number of specimens analysed.
  No count is spoken and none is printed.
- The band's thickness and position are schematic; they carry no units and must not be
  read as an effect size.
- Sulfur and strontium are introduced here but their 355 s+ interpretation (bedrock
  weathering) belongs to the next passage and is **not** covered by this section.
- No scientific review has been done of this plate against the 2019 paper. The footer
  states no values are depicted; that is a limit, not a safeguard.
