# Mammoth vegetation interaction — development scene

This is a 12-second native-3D **illustrative test**, not a filmed or verified mammoth behaviour and not a finished shot. An experimental head and trunk rig reaches toward a procedural grass clump, pauses as the clump bends, then withdraws. The camera widens during the action. The plant motion is hand-authored; neither contact nor the blades are simulated. The animal does not actually pick up or eat the plant. The trunk/plant surface fit, body stiffness, fixed feet, approximate two-joint trunk deformation, potential head/body seams, habitat, animation timing, shadows and overall appearance all need substantial artistic and anatomical review. The SDPM asset's age and rights/provenance remain unresolved; see `../mammoth-rig/README.md` before any distribution. Ground material incorporates the previously generated tundra texture (`../assets/tundra.jpg`) on real 3D geometry. A directional shadow map replaces the prior graphic blob shadow in this scene.

From the repo root, `node production/extinct-life/longform-mammoth/cinematic-rebuild/foraging/inspect.mjs` saves stills to ignored cache. It checks deterministic seeks, browser errors, and closest *sampled vertex-to-vertex distances* between the deformed trunk-tip and the grass mesh at selected instants (below 3 cm at 3–5s; over 30 cm at 8s). This does not establish continuous collision, credible contact, soft-body physics or visual quality. `render-preview.mjs` produces a silent 30fps local review, not a completed film. Existing cache files may be stale if source changes; render again after changes. Narration timing, sound, scene transitions and continuous human review have not been completed.

`mix_preview.py` combines that local review with a provisional excerpt of the existing narration/score draft and a quiet filtered single channel of the CC0 wind source. It verifies decoding, frame count and a true-peak ceiling, records hashes in ignored cache, and never outputs a deliverable. Neither the picture edit, wind phase/character, mix, sample-master equivalence nor the spoken alignment has been reviewed by continuous listening or viewing. The sound source is not an Arctic recording.
