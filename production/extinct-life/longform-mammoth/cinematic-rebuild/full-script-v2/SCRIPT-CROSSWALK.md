## Outstanding decisions

- This crosswalk does **not** certify that every sentence or detail of the original was preserved. The listed context details remain unresolved rather than silently dropped. Original numerical details in the draft's source-anchor list that were never spoken (exact heterozygosity, MHC, deletion-base figures and 483 microbial specimens) are not all included in the current narration; source-check before adding any on-screen statistics.
- The two retakes and automatic captions have not had continuous human listening or proofreading; forced alignment with assumed year pronunciations is not an accuracy check.
- No quality mammoth rig, complete 3D scenes, final score/SFX, continuous edit or finished film is supplied by this folder.
