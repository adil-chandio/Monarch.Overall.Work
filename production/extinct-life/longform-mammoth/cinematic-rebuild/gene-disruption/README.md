# Gene-disruption section — the 2017 result in detail (192.733–220.0 s)

This passage names three kinds of change that can disrupt a gene, says the island genome
carried an excess of them, gives one figure (an estimated effective size more than
forty-three times lower), and then immediately restricts that figure: **a genetic
estimate, not a head count**. It closes by explaining what a premature stop and a
deletion actually do.

## Rules this section follows

- **The 43× never appears alone.** The caveat is on screen within two seconds and in the
  same visual block; the figure is never allowed to stand as a headline.
- **No tick field.** An earlier version drew dozens of marks beside each kind of change.
  That reads as a measured count, and the narration gives none — it says "more", not how
  many more. The marks were removed and the claim is made once, in words.
- **No DNA helix, no sequence, no protein fold.** The two diagrams are schematic lines:
  one stops early, one has a gap. Anything more literal would be invented structure.
- **No animal, no diagnosis.** The section states explicitly that nothing here identifies
  what affected any individual animal.

## Limits

- The two line diagrams are **not** drawn to any scale and carry no bases, codons or
  positions. The footer says diagrams are not data.
- `43×` is reproduced exactly as spoken, including its "more than" qualifier; the plate
  does not restate it as a range or add a confidence interval.
- The smell-related-receptor loss is mentioned in one closing line only; the passage that
  develops it is not covered by this section.
- No geneticist has reviewed this plate.
