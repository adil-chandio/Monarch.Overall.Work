# Genomic record gap — visual experiment

A 17-second conceptual 3D chart about the **2024 study's dataset**, not a reconstruction of actual sample locations. It shows the youngest genome at approximately 4,300 calibrated years before present and a separate, later remains record at approximately 4,000 cal BP. The dashed interval marks missing genomic samples in that dataset, not a record of animal numbers or the absence of all evidence. Approximate dates and a schematic rail must not be treated as precise dates of death, evenly spaced specimens, or an exact extinction date. The youngest genome is reported at 4,333 cal BP; the later remains and terminal chronology have dating uncertainties.

Sources: [Dehasque et al. 2024](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(24)00577-4); [Dehasque et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2021.106913) discusses the approximate 4,024 cal BP youngest sample and a modelled 4.2–3.9 ka cal BP extinction interval. These are visual estimates, not a full radiocarbon confidence interval. Audio timing, label placement, interpretability and source science still require review. The scene is a diagram, not complete storytelling and not a finished documentary.
