# One-genome section — what a single comparison can carry (274–305 s)

The 2017 result is the film's first genetic warning and its weakest evidence: **one
island genome against one mainland genome**. The narration says so outright, then says
that seven years later a larger study found a severe bottleneck, a surprising recovery,
and lasting strain. This 31-second section is the hinge between those two claims.

## Structure

| Shot | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0–10.0 | `A CLUE, NOT A LIKENESS`. Two columns: what DNA preserves (ancestry, relatedness, variants) and what it cannot tell us (how the animal looked, how it coped with Arctic conditions). Then: a genome is evidence about inheritance, not a portrait of a living animal |
| 1 | 10.0–20.5 | `ONE ISLAND GENOME, ONE MAINLAND GENOME`. Two panels, each containing a single mark and the words "one genome". `THE 2017 RESULT RAISED A WARNING`, then `A WARNING FROM TWO GENOMES IS A WARNING, NOT A MEASUREMENT` |
| 2 | 20.5–31.0 | `SEVEN YEARS LATER, A LARGER STUDY`. 21 marks, 7 ochre mainland and 14 blue Wrangel; a severe bottleneck and a surprising recovery, with lasting strain not removed. `A larger sample can support a claim the first could only raise` |

## Rules this section follows

- **The emptiness is the argument.** Shot 1 deliberately consists of two nearly empty
  panels. Filling them would destroy the point the narration is making.
- **No 2024 curve here.** `reversal-section` owns the modelled bottleneck curve at 473 s.
  Drawing it at 274 s would make the same model appear twice and look like independent
  corroboration.
- **No portrait of an animal.** The "clue, not a likeness" beat exists precisely to say
  the genome cannot show how the animal looked, so no animal is shown.
- **The sample counts are labelled as following the narration** — 21 = 7 + 14 — and the
  footer says no dates or values are printed, because none is spoken in this passage.

## Limits

- The 21 marks are a schematic sample, not a map, a time axis, or a skull-and-bones
  inventory. Their positions carry no information.
- "Seven years" is the narration's framing of 2017 → 2024 and is shown as a title, not as
  a date on a scale.
- The section does not summarise either paper's statistics and adds no numbers beyond the
  spoken sample counts.
- No geneticist has reviewed this plate.
