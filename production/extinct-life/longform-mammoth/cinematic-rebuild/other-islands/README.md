# Other islands section — St Paul, and why regional clues differ (114–130 s)

Two statements follow each other quickly, and neither should be illustrated literally:

- on **St Paul Island, Alaska**, mammoths lasted until about **5,600 years ago**;
- isotopic changes from different regions give clues about diet and environment,
  **although the details are not the same everywhere**.

## Structure

| Shot | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0–8.6 | `ST PAUL ISLAND, ALASKA`. A span **bounded at one end only**: the dated right edge reads `LASTED UNTIL ABOUT 5,600 YEARS AGO`, the left edge is an open bracket labelled `not dated here`. Then `A LATER LOSS IS A DIFFERENT HISTORY` — the populations did not end together |
| 1 | 8.6–16.0 | `THE DETAILS ARE NOT THE SAME EVERYWHERE`. Three cards naming what each record *is* — a separate island record ending later than Wrangel; the record this film follows most closely; other regions with their own conditions. Then: a pattern from one region cannot be carried straight across to another |

## Decisions worth keeping

- **The open left edge is the honest form of this bar.** The narration gives a terminal
  date and no start date, so a bar of any arbitrary length would invent one. An earlier
  version used a fade-out gradient, which still implied a start; the bracket does not.
- **No isotope series is drawn, at any point.** A first attempt gave each region a
  distinct invented curve, which reads as measured values — precisely the fabrication the
  49-second `chemistry-section` avoids. The cards now name the record instead of plotting
  it, and the plate says so: *no series is drawn here*.
- **No coastline, no map.** Consistent with `isolation-section` and the abandoned
  `island-map` approach: this production does not trace a palaeoshoreline.
- **St Paul's date is never used as evidence about Wrangel.** The second beat states the
  opposite: a date on one island is not a statement about the others.

## Limits

- 5,600 years is reproduced as spoken ("about"), with no calibration detail, no material
  and no dating method.
- The three region cards are **presentation only**; they do not correspond to any table of
  samples or sites.
- This section covers only 114–130 s. The isotope interpretation that resumes at ~345 s
  is handled by `chemistry-section`, which must be checked for continuity against this
  plate — the same subject is being introduced here and qualified there.
- No isotope specialist or archaeologist has reviewed this plate.
