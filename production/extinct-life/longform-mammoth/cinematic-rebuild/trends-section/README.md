# Two trends section — the 2024 result and its limits (577–629.1 s)

This passage makes three moves, and each one narrows the previous:

1. a difference in how much sequence is readable **could be mistaken** for a difference
   between the animals, so the bias is tested before anything is attributed to them;
2. after accounting for it, **two opposing trends** appear — the highest-impact variants
   became less common (consistent with purging), moderately harmful ones accumulated
   (consistent with drift outpacing selection);
3. these are **predicted effects, not medical examinations**, and **neither finding by
   itself tells us what caused the extinction**.

The plate is built as a test register, then a two-direction diagram, then a record that
visibly stops short of the final generations.

## Structure

| Shot | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0–11.0 | `THE DIFFERENCE COULD BE IN THE DATA`. Two cards: before the test, after the test. Without testing that bias the result would be unreadable |
| 1 | 11.0–28.5 | `AFTER ACCOUNTING FOR THOSE DIFFERENCES, TWO TRENDS`. Two arrows on a shared axis, pointing opposite ways: `BECAME LESS COMMON` / `ACCUMULATED`, each with its one-line explanation. Then `PREDICTED EFFECTS — NOT MEDICAL EXAMINATIONS` |
| 2 | 28.5–38.5 | `NO CLEAR ACCELERATING COLLAPSE`. A fixed-mark band for the sampled genomes, and a dashed empty box for the final generations that are **not in the larger dataset**. A population can recover demographically and still carry lasting strain |
| 3 | 38.5–52.07 | `NEITHER FINDING, BY ITSELF`. Three cards — purging, accumulation, the extinction — with the last marked `not identified here`. Then `HERE IS THE MISSING WITNESS`, handing to the coverage and genomic-gap passages |

## Rules this section follows

- **The bias test comes first, on screen.** This is the passage where a viewer is most
  likely to read raw differences as biological ones; the structure refuses that order.
- **The two trends are never given magnitudes.** Both arrows are the same length and the
  same weight. Only direction is shown — a longer arrow would invent an effect size.
- **"Predicted" is stated in the plate, not only in the voice-over**, and the footer
  repeats it.
- **No damage, no meltdown, no sick animal.** "Genomic collapse" appears only inside a
  negation ("no clear accelerating genomic collapse"), which is how the narration says it.
- **The empty box is the ending.** The final generations are drawn as an absence, so the
  section cannot be read as a complete history.

## Limits

- "Purging" and "drift outpacing selection" are the narration's shorthand for statistical
  results; the plate does not show effect sizes, significance, or model details.
- The sampled-genome band is a **schematic span**, not a time axis with dates. No start or
  end date is printed because none is spoken here.
- The dashed final-generations box does not imply a known duration for the gap.
- No population geneticist has reviewed this plate.
