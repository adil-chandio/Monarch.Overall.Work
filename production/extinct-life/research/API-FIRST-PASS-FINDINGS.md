## Why raw search numbers still need screening

- The Homotherium query's largest result was a roughly 5.3-million-view Joe Rogan/Colossal interview clip about bringing back saber-toothed cats. It is not interchangeable with a 55-second specimen explainer.
- The Vasuki sample included mythology/religious content and a dinosaur-titled result. Those are not clean evidence for scientific fossil-snake demand.
- Broad frozen-animal searches included dinosaur/Antarctica novelty titles. Scientific accuracy, topic fit and the actual video content must be checked before selecting any as a production reference.

No claim that all million-view search hits are relevant, scientifically sound or suitable to copy. Shorts and long-form results have not yet been fully format-classified or compared on equal-age/channel-normalised terms.
