## Remaining weaknesses — not silently marked solved

1. **Low-information holds remain.** In particular, parts of the mineral explanation (approximately 02:12–02:19), proxies (04:10–04:39), early gaps (05:39–05:46) and integration (06:01–06:16) retain long restrained compositions. Internal operations and labels improve specificity, but preserving the original narration duration does not by itself solve editorial pacing. Do not claim every former hold was eliminated.
2. **Encounter mastodon is overly close around 04:51–05:02.** The foreground body/head crops heavily during this approach. It is a visible composition weakness, not a missing encoded frame. Named-species bounding-box gates do not certify this later approach. A further shot redesign is needed to call full-body framing consistently resolved throughout the film.
3. **Stylised character performance remains limited.** Toy-like shapes, simplified wing articulation, restrained secondary action and some stiff/contact-light animal motion remain. Improved clearance and rodent detail do not equal a new professional anatomical rig or physically validated locomotion.
4. **Environment still reads procedural.** Repeated tree forms, sparse backdrop foliage and simplified water/terrain remain visible. Additional plant forms and cutaways improve variety, not photorealistic art direction.
5. **Typography is not universally certified.** Main labels read in the inspected HD frames, but qualifiers are smaller and naming cuts are short. Contact-sheet visibility is not mobile-device readability certification, nor an every-frame label/subject-overlap test.
6. **Opening/operator continuity remains schematic.** The scoop/transfer and research-page sequences are coherent in the sampled motion strips, but do not establish tactile hand/contact realism or continuous embodied walking in every scene. Scientific explanations deliberately use observer views.
7. **Review coverage has limits.** No manual every-frame anatomy inspection, external scientific review, retention study, continuous full-master watch/listen or owner sign-off was performed. Automated decode, per-frame metrics, sampled images and loudness measurements must not be conflated with these.
