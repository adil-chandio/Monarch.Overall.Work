## Metaphor checks

- **Delivery with damaged labels:** analogy for transport and identification difficulty, not a literal postal or molecular mechanism. Its transport/time limitations are spoken immediately in B02.
- **Instruction-book fragments:** explicitly mixed, damaged and incomplete, never an intact genome. Its purpose is to distinguish information recovery from organism recovery.
- **Picture/focus/record:** inference metaphors. B06 explicitly calls the moving scene a reconstruction; it does not claim a recovered photograph.
- **Handful in the title:** an inviting scale metaphor, not a statement that this study used a single scoop or that every soil sample contains this evidence.
- **The lost forest/ancient lives:** the imagined ancient community, not a claim that modern Greenland has no organisms or that living hare/reindeer lineages globally vanished.

## What changed from v2 after the expanded script study

1. Open with animal → missing body → molecular material → mismatched landscape, rather than delaying the concrete puzzle.
2. Move transport, time averaging and deposit-age limits beside the first explanation of environmental DNA. They become rules of the investigation, not late corrections.
3. Turn mineral preservation into a solution with a consequence: harder extraction. This creates a causal bridge to the next problem.
4. Frame identification as eliminating alternative explanations, not a magical match animation.
5. Make the community the second major reveal; the mastodon is not followed by a bigger monster.
6. Put the beautiful imagined scene after the confidence-building work and label its boundary in the same block.
7. Combine missing evidence and complementary methods into one meaningful question, avoiding a late methods appendix.
8. Close the opening contradiction: the animal does not need to fit the present environment because the environment changed. End at the original material rather than adding several contemporary lessons.

These are editorial applications of research mechanisms, not demonstrated retention improvements. The eight blocks total 1,048 English whitespace-counted words. Natural pacing, silence, pronunciation and the translated runtime still require fresh recording and measurement. The older v2 narration, old timeline and rejected films are not v3 assets.
