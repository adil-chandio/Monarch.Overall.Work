## Source anchors actually consulted

**S1 — primary study:** Kjær et al., *A 2-million-year-old ecosystem in Greenland uncovered by environmental DNA*, Nature 612, 283–291 (2022). DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05453-y ; full article https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05453-y . Abstract, Main, Geological age, DNA preservation, Kap København metagenomes, figures 3–4 and DNA/pollen/macrofossil comparison were consulted for this revision. This is not a claim that all supplements were independently reanalysed.

**S2 — museum explanation:** James Ashworth, Natural History Museum, 7 December 2022: https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/news/2022/december/worlds-oldest-dna-represents-two-million-year-old-ecosystem.html . Useful accessible corroboration of setting, earlier plant evidence, sampling and preservation. Its historical record-setting headline is not adopted as a present-day claim.

**S3 — interpretive limits:** the mixed depositional setting and methodological limitations in S1, combined with explicitly identified conservative inference. These limits are not separate measurements of each recovered fragment's journey.

## Language/pronunciation note

The spoken script uses **Kap Copenhagen Formation** as an English-friendly reading of **Kap København Formation**. This does not identify a different locality. Final captions/source notes should retain the correct written form. Mastodon and mammoth are not interchangeable.

## Metaphor versus data

- Shredded identification guides and the damaged sentence are analogies for mixed origins, partial information and information loss. They are not literal biochemistry.
- The representative fragment's continuous camera journey is a storytelling device connecting processes; it is not a tracked molecule from the actual excavation.
- Illustrative extraction/sequence displays are not the paper's raw reads or measured certainty.
- The forest and animals are reconstructions. The study supports a community inference, not one exact landscape photograph or encounter.
- The ending explicitly denies a literal memory in the ground. It describes layers, chemistry and surviving material rather than mystical storage.
