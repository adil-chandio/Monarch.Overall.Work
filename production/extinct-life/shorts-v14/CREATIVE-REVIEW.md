# Creative acceptance — open review items

This Short is a **review candidate**, not a certified fulfilment of “equal or superior to Zack D. Films.” Technical completion is separate from creative acceptance.

## Completed author checks before final encode

- The 102-word approved narration is preserved; no playback speed-up used. Captions cover the exact text in order.
- Native portrait camera, not a landscape crop. First-pass head/tusk cropping and oversized sediment/reference objects were corrected after opened still review.
- Source original animal meshes, with native deformation; no copied creator characters, visuals, footage or sound.
- Short disconnected DNA illustrations; deposit age qualified as approximate; North Greenland locality retained; partial reference comparison rather than a complete genome/confidence percentage.
- Forest and animal scenes explicitly marked imagined reconstructions. Molecular/reference/sediment views marked schematic.
- Narration-led original instrumental bed and designed sediment/air/contact sound layers. Final loudness will be measured from encoded audio, not assumed from source levels.

## Remaining visual/creative limits

- Animal sculpt remains visibly stylized/procedural. Ear shape, tusk detail, skin/fur and movement are not studio character-animation quality and have not been independently anatomically validated. This must not be sold as an exact mastodon species portrait.
- Wide-shot materials use direct-lit Phong; soft authored contact-occlusion planes replace expensive dynamic shadow maps. This preserves full native output resolution/frame sampling but reduces lighting realism.
- Main environmental shots have restrained motion rather than a complex fully rigged animal performance. Reference alignment is an abstract educational graphic, not a replication of the study's sequencing software or actual alignment results.
- Some macro objects are simplified to remain readable on a phone. SFX are illustrative designs, not recordings of molecular processes.
- Footage-level competitor camera/edit/SFX study was not possible from metadata/transcripts. No claim that all Zack videos were watched or that quality parity has been demonstrated.

## Owner review needed

Watch the actual encoded MP4 on a phone and listen both with headphones and small speakers. Judge: first-two-second interest, subject readability, scientific expectation, caption timing, voice clarity, music taste, SFX balance, motion stiffness and whether the full-film bridge feels earned. Objective decoding/loudness tests cannot settle those subjective questions.
