## Mechanism-level breakdown, not invented frame timestamps

### Stitches
1. Immediate physical action: needle draws wound edges together.
2. Static closure becomes a biological process: inflammation/repair.
3. Connective tissue and new blood vessels change the state.
4. A clear endpoint: wound joined; stitches removed.

**Transfer:** Start with a concrete missing-body problem; show a visible process that changes the evidence, not rotating shapes that leave understanding unchanged. Do not borrow gore or skin simulation unrelated to the film.

### Splinter
1. Familiar default solution: pull it out.
2. Obstacle: fragment too deep to reach.
3. Mechanism: swelling/pressure is described.
4. Qualified outcome: “sometimes,” “if you're lucky,” “could.”

**Transfer:** Our sediment/DNA visual has a preservation obstacle. Mineral association MAY help; it is not a magical invulnerable capsule. Keep the qualification with the explanation. This is a story-structure transfer, not an endorsement of the splinter medical claim.

### Hair splinter
1. Counterintuitive small object can penetrate skin.
2. Occupation-specific context makes the event concrete.
3. Physical mechanism explains entry.
4. Conditional consequence explains why it matters.

**Transfer:** Tiny fragments need a clear scale transition and concrete location. Use North Greenland sediment, then a labeled microscopic schematic—not an unlabelled molecular world that implies exact filmed events.

### Bone marrow / blood cells
1. Slice through a familiar object to reveal a hidden system.
2. Follow a specific entity through development.
3. Shape change gives an observable new function.
4. Change environment and continue the path into circulation.

**Transfer:** Exterior clue → sediment cutaway → molecular fragment → mineral association → reference comparison → ecological inference. The camera follows an idea across scales; it need not orbit every object.

## Animation, camera and sound requirements we authored

These are OUR design choices; not claimed measured Zack settings.

- Native portrait camera and full-frame 1080×1920 rendering, not a crop of the long film.
- Direct subject entrance, no production logo or empty intro.
- Six explanatory visual roles: organism, sediment, damaged fragments, mineral surface, partial reference alignment, ecological reconstruction. Separate verification and closing film-bridge beats.
- Native mesh deformation for animal breathing/head/ear/trunk and modest movement; camera dolly on the organism and forest. Avoid strong walking on unvalidated foot-contact rigs.
- Procedural coat/skin surface detail; warm key/cool rim separation, depth/fog and individually instanced leaves rather than large ball-shaped crowns. Final wide shots use direct-lit Phong and authored soft contact occlusion, not dynamic cast-shadow maps. Per-strand line geometry is disabled in the final wide shots for software-render portability.
- Sediment material and mineral macro surfaces are illustrative. No invented instrument precision, DNA confidence percentage, full recovered genome, cloned animal, or exact ancient species portrait.
- DNA fragments move toward a mineral surface; reference fragments move into a partial schematic alignment. No full genomic “unlock” or resurrection transition.
- Narration drives the edit. Voice remains at natural generated speed; actual narration necessitated a 50-second export rather than the initial 44–48-second estimate.
- Own original instrumental bed and own synthesized illustrative Foley; no competitor soundtrack, cloned creator voice, copied character mesh, logos or footage.
- Filtered transition air at scene changes, granular sediment texture, sparse contact ticks during alignment, restrained forest air. These are illustrative designed sounds, not actual molecular noises or expedition recordings.
- Music is ducked below narration. Objective loudness/true-peak/stream QC does not substitute for a human listening review.

## Own-render iteration findings

The first portrait still pass exposed head/tusk cropping, oversized sediment/reference graphics and excessively simplified canopy shapes. These were not accepted as final. Camera distances/orientation were corrected, the entire animal brought inside the portrait frame, sediment edges filled, and canopy replaced with individually instanced leaf geometry. Native full-resolution frames were opened during this process. Initial stills are scratch material, not deliverables.

Remaining limitations must not be hidden: this is procedural/stylized 3D, not a studio character/cloth/fluid simulation; animal anatomy was not independently expert-validated; the reference alignment uses abstract colored bases rather than measured study sequence data; true competitor frame-level quality parity is unverified. Owner acceptance remains open, and no public posting is authorised or performed.
