Two-million-year-old DNA from Greenland’s sediment helped reveal a lost community—including evidence of a mastodon lineage. How can broken genetic fragments tell us so much without a skeleton?

Extinct Life follows the clues from frozen dirt to an ancient landscape: how environmental DNA can survive, how researchers compare fragments, and why a match is not always an exact species identification.

This is an animated science explainer about research reported in 2022—not a newly announced 2026 discovery, real expedition footage, or an animal brought back to life. Wildlife appearances, behaviour and simultaneous co-occurrence are illustrative. DNA symbols are conceptual, not microscope images. These fragments do not provide a complete mastodon genome or an exact recovered portrait.
