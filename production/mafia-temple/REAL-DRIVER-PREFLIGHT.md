# Real running driver research — preparation

Status: **preflight requested, not a production-approved shot**. This record describes the input, not a claim of pose accuracy, likeness or final output quality.

- The 60 test clips of [ASPset-510](https://github.com/anibali/aspset-510/tree/b4f6a70589ebd47abdf0e48d1ff90e8495bb0be5), which its creators dedicate to CC0, were scanned on GitHub Actions run `42516961380`. Test archive MD5 `797eca0da0ac76746a8df7beaf8d2ab6` matched Internet Archive metadata. Five frames per clip were visually screened; subjects were frequently distant, with multiple people and sports actions rather than an unobstructed side-view run. This test archive was not selected as the motion driver.
- Stock candidate: [Eduardo Holanda, Pexels 32740423](https://www.pexels.com/video/man-running-outdoors-on-open-road-32740423/), downloaded rendition 1440×2560, 30 fps, 467 frames, SHA-256 `fef029f40687ee924f0eaef6e3da932485441db4b9a3c992780121b12507e28d`. All 49 sampled frames from 0.25 through 3.25 seconds were visually checked, mirrored to face right and resized to 480×832. There is a visible full-body runner, clear legs and changing footfalls, though arms partially occlude against the torso and the view is slightly from behind rather than a perfect side profile. This is a real performance; it is not, by itself, a guarantee of reliable pose detection or physics. Retiming is nearest-frame resampling from 30 to 16 fps, not slow motion.
- The stock source stays on the private runtime under `/tmp`, is removed after execution, and is not sent through the reference encoder. Only detected pose controls feed the motion model; the source person's appearance is **not** the character. Source frames do not enter the film or generated audit outputs. [Pexels usage rules](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042332714-What-are-the-rules-for-using-Pexels-photos-or-videos) prohibit depicting identifiable people engaging in criminal activities and prohibit implied endorsement. This pose-only research is not a blanket legal clearance for a final film: ensure no identifiable stock performer is represented, and review rights/consent and any ancillary model terms before publication.
- Reference: previously published generated `shot-04-identity-study-b.jpg`. No original user photo or private derived 3D texture is uploaded. `align_to=ref` changes the pose control to the reference, rather than cropping the reference to the pose as in the prior test. The reference remains intact, but skeleton displacement/cropping and face/hand control still need manual review.
- The pipeline first runs a private GPU **pose-only preflight** and checks all 49 retargeted control frames before starting an approved single 49-frame model render. Pose-only stage does not load the transformer, T5, or VAE. A full film is not authorized by preflight and must not be claimed complete.
- Screening used temporary public-dataset and Pexels contact sheets, relayed as unreferenced GitHub blobs for review because the sandbox cannot reach GitHub's artifact CDN. **Git blobs on a public repository are not private**; source clips and user-original/private assets are never relayed. Generated references and generated pilot results may be relayed under the user's existing permission, but are not automatically committed as approved final media.
