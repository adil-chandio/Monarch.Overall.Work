# Real running driver research — preparation

Status: **preflight requested, not a production-approved shot**. This record describes the input, not a claim of pose accuracy, likeness or final output quality.

- The 60 test clips of [ASPset-510](https://github.com/anibali/aspset-510/tree/b4f6a70589ebd47abdf0e48d1ff90e8495bb0be5), which its creators dedicate to CC0, were scanned on GitHub Actions run `42516961380`. Test archive MD5 `797eca0da0ac76746a8df7beaf8d2ab6` matched Internet Archive metadata. Five frames per clip were visually screened; subjects were frequently distant, with multiple people and sports actions rather than an unobstructed side-view run. This test archive was not selected as the motion driver.
- Stock candidate: [Eduardo Holanda, Pexels 32740423](https://www.pexels.com/video/man-running-outdoors-on-open-road-32740423/), downloaded rendition 1440×2560, 30 fps, 467 frames, SHA-256 `fef029f40687ee924f0eaef6e3da932485441db4b9a3c992780121b12507e28d`. All 49 sampled frames from 0.25 through 3.25 seconds were visually checked, mirrored to face right and resized to 480×832. There is a visible full-body runner, clear legs and changing footfalls, though arms partially occlude against the torso and the view is slightly from behind rather than a perfect side profile. This is a real performance; it is not, by itself, a guarantee of reliable pose detection or physics. Retiming is nearest-frame resampling from 30 to 16 fps, not slow motion.
- The first private Kaggle preflight failed with HTTP 403 while fetching an input; no pose extraction or generation occurred (`42517966143`). The replacement path downloads and checksum-verifies the public stock video on the Actions runner, places it in a **private** Kaggle dataset, verifies its private status before submission, then mounts it read-only in the private runtime. A temporary copy under `/tmp` is removed after execution. It is not sent through the reference encoder. Only detected pose controls feed the motion model; the source person's appearance is **not** the character. Source frames do not enter the film or generated audit outputs. [Pexels usage rules](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042332714-What-are-the-rules-for-using-Pexels-photos-or-videos) prohibit depicting identifiable people engaging in criminal activities and prohibit implied endorsement. This pose-only research is not a blanket legal clearance for a final film: ensure no identifiable stock performer is represented, and review rights/consent and any ancillary model terms before publication.
- Reference: previously published generated `shot-04-identity-study-b.jpg`. No original user photo or private derived 3D texture is uploaded. Pose preflight `42519157035` completed with `align_to=ref` and preserved the full reference including both shoes. However, visual inspection of controls 0–41 plus sparse controls 42 and 48 revealed very stretched shoulders and hands and unstable arm/leg locations, so this pose is **rejected for rendering**. The remaining seven-frame contact sheet did not arrive through the annotation relay; do not claim all 49 retargeted frames reviewed. The graph's segment-wise `align_to_reference` retargeter is a plausible source of distortion with near-profile/occluded landmarks, but the precise cause is not proven.
- The next pose-only preflight tests `align_to=none` (no per-limb retarget and no reference warp) and a uniform 1.45× crop/reposition of the source. Preliminary inspection of 28 local crop previews found that a centered crop clipped some trailing feet; shifting the target center right improves the margin but must be checked against the actual pose outputs. Face landmarks from the stock performer are disabled. This is a targeted hypothesis, not an automatic quality pass.

Pose-only stage does not load the transformer, T5, or VAE. A full film is not authorized by preflight and must not be claimed complete.
