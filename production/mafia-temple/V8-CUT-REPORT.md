# Monarch night temple — V8 cut

**Status: measured engineering cut. Not creative acceptance, not a finished
likeness film, not rights-cleared.** Read `KNOWN-LIMITS.md` before showing this
to anyone as a deliverable.

- File: `render-v8/monarch-temple-v8.mp4` — 1280×720, 24 fps, **480 frames /
  20.000 s**, h264 high, AAC 48 kHz. It is untracked and private by
  `.gitignore`; it is regenerated by the command at the bottom, never committed.
- Sound: `render-v8/sound_v8.wav` (also untracked).
- Previous cut it replaces: `monarch-temple-native-review.mp4` (same length).

## What V8 did differently

Every item below is a measured repair with a number attached, or it was not
done. The full per-shot numbers are in `qc/out/v8/render_log.json`; the A/B
numbers are in `qc/out/v8/compare.json`.

| Task | Method | Measured result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Judge the footage honestly | `qc/measure_shots.py` decodes all 26 generated clips | 8 of 26 clips are frozen for ≥10 consecutive frames; `s01`, `s01a`, `s05b` are unusable (29-frame freezes) |
| Pick the best native clip per shot | measured motion + 4-frame visual sheets | `wan4-*` (768×448 max native 512×288) replaces the frame-jumping choices |
| Frame-accurate timeline | `retime_indices` only *drops* frames | exactly 480 frames, **0 duplicated pairs**, one 5-frame dissolve |
| Kill flicker | per-shot median deflicker, auto-vetoed if it does not help | worst luma step 29.50 → **13.81** (−53%) |
| Kill camera shake | track the rigid residual, cancel it, clamp to 10 px | S2 5.39 → **3.26 px**, S3 4.12 → **2.13 px** RMS |
| More real detail | ESPCN x2 learned upscale instead of bicubic | +2% to +44% high-frequency energy per clip (median ≈ +20%) |
| Exposure continuity | per-shot gain matching, clamped to ±12% | no visible exposure jumps across the four cuts |
| The eye effect | Haar face box → anatomical pupil prior → validated dark-pupil snap → patch tracking | **97/97 frames tracked**, both eyes symmetric, ramped over 1.4 s |
| Night look | teal shadows, vignette, per-frame grain, 1.05 contrast, black lift | applied in float32 before encode |
| Sound | existing 20 s mix + cut impacts + riser + picture-gated sub pulse | **−25.6 → −15.7 LUFS**, true peak −10.3 → −1.3 dBTP (source was clipping at +1.3) |

The eye glow and every other effect **fails closed**: if the pupil pair is not
verified on a frame, nothing is drawn. That is why there is no red blob on the
temple gate or on a turned-away head.

## What V8 honestly is *not*

- **The likeness is still approximate and unverified.** No face-descriptor or
  human comparison has accepted it, and the generated face drifts across the
  turn. `film_tools` cannot fix identity.
- **Anatomy defects remain**: the running shot still has soft/merged hands and
  feet leaving the ground without contact shadows. Stabilisation makes the
  frame steadier; it does not make the stride correct.
- **Sharpness is not 3D detail.** The upscale adds learned texture at 1280×720;
  the source clips are 512×288–768×448. `high_freq_energy` rising 19.4 → 27.8 is
  a texture measure, **not** proof of resolution beyond the source, and honest
  1080p is not available from this source without a real render.
- **The 3D blockout is not in this film.** The Blender route
  (`3d-pilot/`, `PROGRESS.md`) is still a generic untextured stand-in. It was
  not used here, and it is not ready to be.
- **Rights are not cleared** for the visual model or its outputs. CC0 audio
  provenance is recorded in `audio/SOURCES.md`; the added synthesis layers are
  generated in-repo and add no licensing surface. This is not legal clearance.
- **Nobody has listened to the audio or watched it at full speed.** The
  loudness numbers are engineering measurements. Footstep sync is not claimed;
  the added low pulse follows *measured picture motion*, not recorded footsteps.

## Reproduce it

```bash
bash production/mafia-temple/qc/setup_env.sh && source production/mafia-temple/qc/.env
cd production/mafia-temple

# 1. measure the sources (writes qc/clip_metrics.json)
$PY qc/measure_shots.py --clips clips --output qc/clip_metrics.json

# 2. master the sound (writes qc/out/v8/sound_v8.wav + sound_log.json)
$PY qc/make_sound_v8.py --source monarch-mafia-temple-20s.mp4 \
    --out qc/out/v8/sound_v8.wav

# 3. render the picture (~2.5 min on 2 CPU cores, no GPU)
$PY qc/render_film_v8.py --out qc/out/v8/monarch-temple-v8.mp4 \
    --superres "$ESPCN" --audio qc/out/v8/sound_v8.wav

# 4. prove the deltas
$PY qc/compare_cuts.py --a monarch-temple-native-review.mp4 \
    --b qc/out/v8/monarch-temple-v8.mp4 --json qc/out/v8/compare.json

# 5. tests (30 offline checks, incl. the 480-frame timeline contract)
$PY -m unittest production/mafia-temple/qc/test_film_tools.py
```

## The single next blocker

Not another filter pass. The source is the ceiling: 512×288 generated frames
and an unverified likeness. The next real step is a **GPU render of the final
scene at 1080p with a reference-matched character**, or the owner accepting this
as an internal study. Everything a CPU-only sandbox can honestly add to this
footage has now been added.
