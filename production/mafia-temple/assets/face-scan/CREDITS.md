# Face scan asset

`LeePerrySmith.glb` and `Map-COL.jpg` are the Lee Perry-Smith head scan, the
reference model distributed with three.js
(`examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/`).

- Author: Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite Realities Centre, Trento, Italy
- Licence: **CC BY 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
- Changes: none to the files. The renderer rescales the mesh, sets the eye line
  at the origin, re-lights it and paints an emissive red iris over the texture.

The 20-second cut carries no end card, so attribution is given here and in
`V8-3D-REPORT.md` (section *Attribution*) and in the release notes.
