# Draft sound assets and provenance

This is a proposed **research mix**, not an approved film soundtrack. Its cue timing comes from motion-capture guide frames and must be matched to the final visible foot contacts; mixing, scene transitions and the music choice require listening and creative review. Source binaries are deliberately not committed here.

- `Searching.ogg` by **yd**, https://opengameart.org/node/10684 — the source page lists CC0 1.0. A copy is available at `assets/audio/music/searching.ogg` in `powerbassist/World-simulator`, commit `db513f76befe754b78acd9e244eaa35ce3b400bf`.
- Stone footsteps by **Fantozzi**, sliced by **qubodup**, https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone — source page lists CC0 1.0. Copies at `assets/audio/steps/stone/stone_01.wav` through `stone_06.wav` in `evanlin23/blocky-stories`, commit `f0dd86c4563fa5600bdb898bd3548d99d19dfefe`; their repo documents mono conversion and low-pass.
- Rain by **Kresiek The Furry**, https://opengameart.org/content/amb-rain-loop-1 — source page lists CC0 1.0. Copy `assets/audio/amb/rain.wav` in the same pinned `blocky-stories` repo, shortened and filtered there.
- Wind by **Thimras**, https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences — source page lists CC0 1.0. Copy `assets/audio/amb/wind.wav` in the same pinned `blocky-stories` repo, shortened and filtered there.

All listed source pages and the repository manifests were reviewed during this research. CC0 is separate from the research-only visual model and driver restrictions.
