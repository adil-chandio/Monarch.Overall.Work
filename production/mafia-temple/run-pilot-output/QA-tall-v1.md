# Higher-resolution running test — research QA, not final

24 frames at 24 fps, 576×1024; inference 480.3 seconds. Eight representative frames plus frame 9 at full resolution were inspected. Greater source resolution improves apparent detail, and smaller hand guides reduced the elongated-finger problem, but the character remains an approximate likeness. At frame 0 there is a detached dark shoe-like object; feet are cut by the side edge on several frames, and a thin black tether-like artifact appears between the hands around frames 9–12. Hands and arm poses remain unnatural, the foot/shadow relationship is inconsistent, and the background remains essentially static. One second is not the requested running shot. This test does not qualify for final delivery and has unresolved model rights.
