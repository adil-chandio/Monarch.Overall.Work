# Full-length occlusion-aware running test — research QA, not final

72 frames at 24 fps, 576×1024, 1292.8 seconds inference. Twelve full-frame samples and a contact sheet covering all 72 foot crops were inspected. The long flesh-colored bridge between hands is generally reduced compared with v4, but hands remain malformed in places, face identity is still approximate, shoe shapes and ground contact fluctuate, and the first frame has a skin-like sleeve. The initial frame was removed in a **local-only** composite experiment and one end frame duplicated to preserve duration; this hides that one discontinuity, not the underlying anatomical or contact problems.

The pose-segmented 2.5D tracking composite was checked at 12 sampled frames. It still lacks verified matched reflections, physically grounded feet and true 3D parallax, and the pose-driven shadow is approximate. The test remains research-only with unresolved model/driver licensing. It is not a final running shot.
