## 4. Pin kaise karein (practical steps)

1. Comments on rakhein: Settings -> Comments and ratings [1](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404?hl=en); comments off/limited hone par pin kaam nahi karega [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000964?hl=en).
2. Channel par 'Advanced features' enable karein — pin karne ke liye zaroori hai, aur poore channel par dikhne me 24 ghante tak lag sakte hain [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000964?hl=en).
3. Apna comment post karein, phir uske three-dot menu -> Pin -> PIN [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000964?hl=en); ya Studio -> Community -> Published -> More -> Pin [4](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9482367?hl=en).
4. Shorts ke comments bhi Studio ke 'Content type' filter mein hain, to pin wahan se bhi kiya ja sakta hai [4](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9482367?hl=en).
5. Mobile par viewer ko comment section expand karna padta hai; pinned comment 'view all' kholne par sabse pehle dikhta hai. Comment preview mein dikhna guaranteed nahi hai [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000964?hl=en).
6. Isliye pehli line chhoti aur sabse important rakhi hai — sawal — taake truncated preview me bhi kaam kare.
7. Ek hi templated comment ko kai videos par repeat na karein; naya comment pin karne par purana apne aap unpin ho jata hai [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000964?hl=en) [4](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9482367?hl=en).

## 5. In cheezon se bachein

- Like/comment/subscribe ke badle inaam, giveaway, coercion, 'sub-for-sub' ya forced engagement [5](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en).
- 'Comment YES', 'tag a friend', 'click the heart' jaisa low-effort repetitive engagement bait [5](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en).
- Aisa hook jo video deliver na kare (malicious clickbait), ya comments me traffic lane ke liye repetitive/deceptive messages [5](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en).
- 'Official NASA', 'NASA approved', NASA endorsement, insignia/logo, ya current astronauts ke naam/likeness ka promotional istemal [9](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) [10](https://www.nasa.gov/collaborating-with-nasa/strategic-partnerships/).
- Hashtag stuffing, unrelated tags, ALL CAPS, emoji-spam ya description wale descriptive tags ko comment me chipkana [6](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en).
- 'No CGI' / '100% real' jaise unverified authenticity claims — workspace me human_full_motion_review=false hai.
- Guaranteed viral / retention / views / RPM ya audience-result ka koi claim.
