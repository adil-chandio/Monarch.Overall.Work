## Chunk 06

A quick honest note about the clips. Every one of them comes from a Wikimedia Commons file page with a recorded licence, artist, source address and checksum. The small line at the bottom of the screen is that source. We are not claiming ownership of somebody else's moment. What is original here is the selection, the order, the replays, the captions and this commentary. That distinction matters. A bare stitch is not a video. A structured, narrated, re-edited sequence is a different piece of work. The rights ledger travels with the project, and a human still needs to review every clip before any real publication.
