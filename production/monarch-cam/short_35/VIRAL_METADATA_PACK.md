## 2. Title A/B variants

Test one variable at a time and compare equivalent post-publication windows.

| Variant | Title | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| A — control | **This Bear Didn’t Need the Door** | Pattern interrupt + factual window action |
| B | **The Window Was Enough** | Shortest curiosity gap |
| C | **Why Bears Keep Checking Cars** | Explanatory / search-friendly |
| D | **One Food Reward Can Teach This** | Mechanism-forward |
| E | **Don’t Teach Bears This Lesson** | Prevention / consequence |

All are under YouTube’s title limit and avoid the false handle-opening claim.

### Deliberately rejected titles

Do **not** use:

- `This Bear Learned to Open Car Doors — Here’s Why`
- `He Used the Handle`
- `Bear Breaks Into Car`
- `Bear Opens Car Door`
- `Wait For It…`
- `Uber Bear` / `Bear Goes Shopping`
- Any title claiming **25M views**, a previous food reward, or this bear’s unrecorded history.

Reason: the selected NPS footage shows window entry/exit, not a readable handle action; invented history and anthropomorphic jokes would break the factual guardrails.

## 5. Posting plan

- Upload the **native 1080×1920** MP4; do not re-export from the horizontal test.
- Use `review/cover.jpg` as the custom cover reference where available.
- Do not schedule a generic “best time” from a blog. Use the channel’s own YouTube Analytics peak active-viewer window; if there is no reliable data, test two comparable windows at least 24 hours apart.
- Publish one title/cover variant first. Do not change title, cover and description simultaneously before you have a baseline.
- Do not call the Short viral before the data exists.

## 6. Measurement after publication

Compare equivalent windows (for example, first 24 h, 3 d and 7 d) against recent Monarch Cam Shorts:

- **Stayed to watch / swiped away**
- **Engaged views**
- **Average view duration**
- **Percentage viewed**
- **Loops / repeat views**
- **Likes, comments, shares and saves**
- **Subscribers gained**
- **Traffic source: Shorts feed vs search vs suggested**

Winning condition: a measurable lift in retention/engagement, not a pre-declared view count. If variant C wins on search but loses on Shorts-feed retention, treat them as different objectives.

---

## 7. Factual and rights guardrails

- No handle pull, door unlocking, latch or “used the handle” claim.
- No claim this individual bear previously received food.
- No visible or implied food reward.
- No bear roar, attack, vehicle-impact or native camera audio claim.
- No reuse of the unrelated 25M-view viral clip.
- No nonexistent related-video or full-episode link.
- No virality, revenue or subscriber guarantee.
- Credits must remain in the description: NPS public-domain picture + the four CC0 audio sources.
