# Factual lock — Laysan albatross courtship dance

- USFWS, *A Laysan Lovesong*: https://www.fws.gov/story/2017-08/laysan-lovesong
  - Young albatrosses gather in twos and threes to **practice courtship dances** for several years.
  - Laysan courtship dances can have **dozens of ritualized movements**.
  - USFWS explicitly calls these gatherings **dance parties**.
  - Once a pair bond forms, the birds **stay bonded for life**.
- Animal Diversity Web, *Phoebastria immutabilis*: https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Phoebastria_immutabilis/
  - Courtship display includes **bill-clicking**, tucking bills under wings, and **pointing bills at the sky simultaneously**.
  - Laysan albatrosses are monogamous and mate for life.
- Commons description: filmed on **Midway Atoll**; video by Ann Bell/USFWS.
