It verifies 25 courtship postures, bill-clicking, bills tucked under wings, simultaneous sky-pointing, monogamy, lifelong mating and pair bonds formed over several years.
- Young albatrosses gather in groups to practice courtship dances for years; USFWS calls them dance parties.
- Laysan courtship dances have dozens of ritualized movements, including bowing, bill-clicking and sky-pointing.
- Once a pair bond forms, the birds stay bonded for life.
- Guardrail: this is general species behavior, not a claim about these two filmed birds' age, pair status or history.
