## 2. Retention-first redesign

| Old 35 s edit | New 20 s edit |
|---|---|
| Hook + window answer by 4.35 s | Hook at 0.00 s, food answer by 3.00 s |
| 70 words | **48 words** |
| Diagram at 16.25 s | Diagram at **5.00 s** |
| “Food conditioning” named at 19.35 s | Badge at **7.00 s**, spoken at **7.20 s**, before the measured leak |
| Prevention at 30.35 s | Prevention begins at **13.60 s** |
| Loop at 30.35–35.00 s | Loop begins at **17.60 s** and holds to 20.00 s |
| 11 scenes / 11 cues | **9 scenes / 12 cues** |
| Caption moved up once | Larger 1040×780 action window, caption at y=1160 |
| Mild cut accents | **2-frame amber cut accents** at 10 pattern-interrupt frames |
| Title had hashtags pasted into it | Clean title; hashtags stay in description |
| Auto cover at 2.20 s | Cover uses the **0.07 s hook frame** |

The first 3 seconds now deliver: **window action → not a ride → food**. The learning loop arrives while attention is still alive. A named `FOOD CONDITIONING` badge appears at 7.0 s and the phrase is spoken at 7.2 s as a deliberate pattern interrupt. The final line holds through the end and returns to the opening window angle.

## 3. Source and factual lock

- Sole picture source: NPS **Bears in Cars**, explicitly public domain.
- Selected windows: 338 s, 18 s, 316 s, 19 s, 340 s, 352 s, 14 s, and a 0.75× 338 s loop.
- Stronger, already-reviewed action: bear at/through the window, bear upright at the window, bear inside the car, bear looking out.
- Audio: the same five verified CC0 sources; no generated bear/vehicle foley.
- No handle opening, door unlocking, food reward, attack, bear vocalisation, native camera audio, or claim about this individual bear’s past.
- No unrelated 25M-view clip, no unverified history, no nonexistent related video.

## 5. Measurement plan

Compare 24 h / 3 d / 7 d against the first Short’s equivalent windows:

- Stayed to watch and swiped away
- Engaged views
- Average percentage viewed
- Average view duration
- Loops / repeat views
- Likes, comments, shares, saves
- Subscribers gained
- Shorts feed vs search vs suggested

Success condition is a **relative lift in retention and engagement**, not a pre-declared view count. If the 20 s edit lifts average percentage viewed but lowers total watch time, judge it as a Shorts-feed retention win, not a direct watch-time comparison.

## 6. Delivery caution

All objective gates, model-assisted review and visual checks are evidence. They are not human listening approval and cannot guarantee audience behavior. The word “viral” describes the design objective only.
