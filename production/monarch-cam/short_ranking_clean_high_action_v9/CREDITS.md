# High Action V9 — source credits and rights boundary

V9 is a separate corrected 60s Short made after the clean V8 MP4 was rejected for weak/static clip quality.
It preserves the clean V8 content-first layout while replacing the source pool and payoff windows with seven
distinct visually curated high-action moments. No clip from the linked YouTube/TikTok reference Short is
downloaded or reused. Older rights-cleared Commons social-origin mirrors are allowed; no currently trending
named-platform clip or exact reference clip is claimed.

Each selected title is re-verified in CI through the Commons API (`extmetadata`), including the original SHA-1,
licence short name, Credit, Categories, ImageDescription and the capital `Restrictions` key. The generated
ledger is `review/verified_sources.json`; the per-title policy record is `review/social_origin_ledger.json`.
Commons-generated VP9/Opus transcodes may be downloaded for large originals; the original API SHA-1, licence,
creator, Credit and source page remain the rights record.

## Boundary

- These are older rights-cleared Commons mirrors of social-origin clips, **not** currently trending
  TikTok / Instagram / YouTube / Reddit / Dailymotion reposts. No current-trending claim is made.
- Only `CC0`, `Public domain` and `CC BY 3.0` sources are allowed. Unverified social reposts, licence-review
  flags, non-free-audio flags, CC BY-SA/NC and fair-use-only claims are rejected.
- Commons metadata is a rights record, not a legal opinion; full legal compliance is not claimed.
- The reference Short was identified by metadata only (`onlymeamro` / `thatoneamro`, `08-ocSDpt7o`, 60s).
  Its frames were not playable/extractable in this environment, so V9 is a corrected high-action rebuild,
  not a frame-by-frame reproduction of that video.
- No human full-motion/native-audio listening, creative approval, sign-off, perfection, retention, virality,
  likes/subscribes or audience result is claimed.
