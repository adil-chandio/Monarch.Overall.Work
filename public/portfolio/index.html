A LETTER FROM ADIL — 31 OCT 2026

March 2024. I bought a second-hand Dell laptop for about 400 dollars — and started a YouTube automation business with it. I found one channel from abroad, studied it frame by frame, built my content around what I learned, and laid down my own strategies. One, two videos — properly viral. Three months in, my first channel was monetized.

TWO MONTHS LATER — THE $700

After the PIN, the paperwork, the waiting — my first earning landed: 700 dollars. It was new money. And I’ll be honest: it corrupted me. The discipline I had built in those first four, five months of grinding — it broke. I slowed down. Back to friends, wandering, movies, enjoyment. And the environment had a hand in it too: some wrong people walked into my life. Their mindset matched mine at the time, so I thought — these are my people, we’ll grow together, I’ll carry them with me. I was the dumb one. I spent a lot of time on them. On one of them, especially.

NO EXCUSES — THE MIRROR PART

I could blame them all day. But I’m the one responsible. I chose it. Everything that happened, happened because I wanted it to. That’s the part I don’t skip.

2025 — THE LOOP

I wasted 2025 the same way: work, quit, work, quit. Some days trading, some days something else, some days nothing at all. And like that, 2025 slipped into 2026.

2026 — AI, AND THE SAME OLD GHOST

In 2026 I started exploring AI — and it felt amazing. Learning, creating, showing off a little creativity: it felt like play. I started properly. But the same bad habit: work hard for a while… then nothing for a while.

OCT 31, 2026 — TODAY

From today: no distractions. Only discipline, consistency, mehnat. Only learning, only growing. Alone if I have to — and with the people who are actually serious. Not with the clowns. I’m 21. I grieve the two, three years I wasted, because I know how much I’m capable of and I know I didn’t do it. That regret is real. But this season of life comes to teach you — and to make you stronger.

WHAT’S NEXT

Soon I’ll build a community — a room for serious people only. It’ll take a little time, because right now I’m running everything from one device: a Techno Spark Go 1. That’s the whole setup. First I build the setup. Then — we cause chaos. Thank you for reading.

— ADIL • 31 OCT 2026 • 3 AM, OBVIOUSLY
