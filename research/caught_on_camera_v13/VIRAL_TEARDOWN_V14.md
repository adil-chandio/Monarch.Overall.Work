## 2. The viral formula (synthesized from real evidence)

1. **0–3 s payoff:** First frame is already moving footage with a readable joke. No intro, no title card, no "welcome back". Doorbell text, Bean face-plant, gorilla eyes — all readable in 1 s.
2. **3–5 s pattern interrupt:** Cut / zoom punch / speed ramp / replay / crop change every 4.5 s. No static composition >4.5 s. FailArmy transcript proves 1–3 s per clip works.
3. **Ordinary setup → impossible timing:** Porch, pool, classroom, maze game — all ordinary. The camera just happens to be there. No script, no studio.
4. **Native audio under short VO:** VO bursts 2.5–4.5 s, then original sound back up. Small noises are often funniest. FailArmy + Ranking Rawr both do this.
5. **Burned-in captions for muted:** Punchline readable without sound. Doorbell text, FailArmy captions, V14 SRT all do this.
6. **Escalation by absurdity:** Not by topic. Skeleton legs → torso → head. Fail 1 → fail 10 → fail 20, each more absurd. V14: 3 rounds, Round 3 fastest.
7. **Challenge / ranking framing:** "Try Not To Laugh", "Ranking", "Top 10" — gives a reason to stay. V14 title keeps "20 Moments" count early.
8. **No dead space:** No static card, no diagram, no analytics essay, no 100 s monologue. Footage is the hook, commentary only points.
9. **Callback storyline:** Hard cut back to clip 1 in Round 3. Tannerites skeleton build is the model: one thread, payoff at end. V14 does this with Bean DIVE callback.

## 3. Why V13 failed (honest)

- V13 contact sheet (`curated_contact_sheet_v13.jpg`) shows: golf shop CCTV (static), parking fail (dark, low-energy), cop vs rat (empty corridor), hang-5 (tiny cyclists), staged fight (night, blurry), bear in house (back of officer), reading driver (empty road), caption fail (woman indoors), parcel CCTV (cars), scarecrow (person in bed), restroom prank (hand on door), animated prank (cartoon), greased pigs (tiny figures), Chaplin (B&W, 1914), 1906 faces (chalk drawing), funny shave (B&W), animal compilation (baby belly rub), boxing cats (B&W grain), boxing dog (B&W trees), dog show (wide shot).
- **Diagnosis:** 8/20 are B&W / vintage / animation — not mazedar to a 2026 viewer. 6/20 are static interiors / empty roads — no visual joke in 1 s. VO referenced cat/dog/bird/squirrel clips that were not in the final cut — false narration. Metadata/QC/contact-sheet was treated as creative approval — it is not.
- **V14 fix:** Replace all 8 vintage/B&W/animation/low-energy clips with genuinely funny modern clips (Bean x3, Chiitan, baby monkey, cat wheel, animals workout, baby elephant, DDR, April Fools, projector prank, scary maze x2, COMEDY, cat grooming, Flip cartoons x2, cat meowing) + keep only 2 V13 keepers that are genuinely funny (parallel parking fail, greased pigs prank). Rewrite VO to match actual clips. Visually audit 0/2.5/5/7.5 s frames before rebuild.

## 5. Editing grammar for V14 (copied from viral evidence, not invented)

- Frame 1: Bean DIVE face-plant, already moving, captions on. No intro/title/method/diagram/static.
- 120 beats × 4.5 s, 3 rounds: ROUND 1 / THE HOOK, ROUND 2 / WHAT HAPPENED, ROUND 3 / ONE MORE TIME. Round labels small overlays over footage.
- Every 4.5 s: cut / zoom punch (192×108 crops) / speed ramp (0.85×–1.3×) / replay / crop change. No static hold >4.5 s.
- VO bursts 2.5–4.5 s, English only, voice-04, native audio ducked under VO (0.30 volume) then back up. Captions burned in (DejaVu Sans 20, MarginV=110, y=h-70 to avoid source overlay overlap).
- Lower-third source attribution only (Wikimedia Commons | licence | short), never full-screen card.
- Final frame still footage (Bean DIVE callback), not static end card.
- 960×540 / 30 fps / H.264 High / AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo / loudnorm -14 LUFS.

**Honest limits:** Technical QC (duration, frames, loudness, SHA, no static hold) is NOT creative approval. No guaranteed views/virality/perfection. No family-safety guarantee. No NASA endorsement. Third-party videos studied, not copied into edit.
