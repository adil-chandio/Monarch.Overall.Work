## What changed vs the rejected V15

- **Clip pool replaced.** V15 reused the V14 20-clip pool and was rejected. V16 uses 20 different Commons files: instant karma drivethru, unexpected hatchlings, a slipping beetle, kittens ×3, a pig race, a rope jump, an FHP kitten rescue, squirrel fishing, owl, red squirrel, bear in a library, cliff jump, muntjac deer, base jump, dirt jump biking, P-42 kittens, red fox pups, young raccoons and a baby tropicbird. Automated comparison against `curated_titles_v13.json` shows zero overlap with the V14/V15 pool.
- **VO rewritten and timeline-aligned.** Chunk 00 is the unchanged V15 intro. Chunks 01–10 are new `voice-05` audio, written for the 20 new clips and aligned to the 9-second-per-clip EDL windows across all three rounds. No V14/V15 clip references remain.
- **Audio design kept.** Native audio 0.60 between VO windows, 0.15 during VO, VO 1.0, final `loudnorm I=-14`. Gaps leave the beetle, kittens, pig race, owl and bird calls audible.
- **Honest boundary.** Direct TikTok / Instagram / Reddit / YouTube acquisition was attempted and abandoned: YouTube downloads failed 18/20 and the two non-YouTube files lacked usable rights metadata, so none are in this pool. Social / viral keywords were used only as Commons search recall and ranking heuristics. **No clip is claimed to be viral, to have originated on a social platform, or to have a view count.** See `V16_PLAN.md`.
