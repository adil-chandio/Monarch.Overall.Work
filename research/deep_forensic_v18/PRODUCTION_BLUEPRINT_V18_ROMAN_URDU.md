## 0. Provisional recommendation — kya choose karne ki tajweez hai

**Working name:** `VISUAL PROOF LAB — Kya Ye Sach Mein Kaam Karega?`

## 9. Safety, rights, originality aur QC

### 9.1 Materials

`plain printer paper`, `ruler`, `tape only if disclosed`, `1 kg calibrated weight / sealed water bottle`, `flat table`, `phone/camera`, `soft catch tray`.

### 9.2 Exclude

Fire, concentrated peroxide, chemicals, glass under load, sharp tools, minors-as-spectacle, animals, competitor clips, unverified life hacks, dangerous replication. Mark Rober ke large-scale chemical experiment ki secondary review me adult/professional safety planning aur ~200°F foam heat ka zikr hai [1](https://watchlyplayer.tv/watchly-safety-review/mark-rober/videos/mark-rober-worlds-largest-elephant-toothpaste-experiment); is liye hamara pilot **kaagaz/weight** ka safe mechanical test hai, chemical stunt nahi. Ye **legal clearance nahi**.

### 9.3 Rights ledger

- Footage: 100% self-shot original (abhi banaya nahi gaya; user approval ke baad hi).
- Voice: own recording ya disclosed TTS; kisi competitor ki voice clone nahi.
- Music/SFX: original ya licence-documented.
- Graphics/maps: self-made simple diagrams; koi third-party map tile tabhi jab rights verify ho.
- AI/synthetic: agar use ho to disclose; koi legal opinion nahi.

### 9.4 Originality / repetition guard

Official YouTube Help ke mutabiq monetised channels ko original/authentic hona chahiye aur mass-produced, generic, repetitive ya template-like content se bachna chahiye [1](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en). Is liye:

- Har episode ka **alag sawal**, **alag failure**, **alag mechanism**.
- Short/medium/long sirf re-cut nahi; har format ka alag promise aur alag proof.
- Long ko unrelated clips ka compilation nahi banana.
- Title/tag/hashtag stuffing nahi.
- Ye policy reading **legal advice ya channel clearance nahi**.

### 9.5 QC checklist (future upload se pehle)

1. User ne provisional niche ya koi aur option **explicitly choose** kiya ho.
2. Original footage + rights ledger complete.
3. Safety review pass: no dangerous chemical / animal / minor-risk / sharp load.
4. Har factual claim visible test se match kare; limitations likhi hon.
5. Koi competitor clip, audio, music, map, transcript text na ho.
6. AI/synthetic disclosure agar applicable; legal clearance claim na ho.
7. Title, description, chapters, tags, captions, thumbnail actual video se match kare.
8. Technical QC: duration, aspect, resolution, audio level, safe zones, caption readability.
9. Human creative review pending; **is report ne koi upload/publish nahi kiya**.

### 9.6 Measurement (sirf future upload ke baad)

- Public: apne views/likes/comments 24h / 7d / 28d.
- Private: retention/CTR/RPM sirf creator ke apne analytics me; is public-API report me nahi.
- Comparison: apne channel baseline se, **high-view-conditioned competitor medians se nahi**.
- Koi guaranteed views, virality, RPM, monetisation outcome ya perfection claim nahi.
