## 1. Pehla kaam (2 minute) — monetization OFF

**Computer / Studio web:**
1. YouTube Studio kholein → left menu **Content**.
2. Is water-wale Short ke aage **Monetization** column par click karein.
3. Dropdown me **Off** chunein.
4. **Save** dabayein. [1](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6332943?hl=en)

**Mobile / Studio app:**
1. YouTube Studio app → bottom **Menu → Content**.
2. Video select karein → **Edit** → **Monetization**.
3. **OFF** chunein → **Back** → **Save**. [2](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522?hl=en)

> Official: *"You can turn off monetization for any videos you've already uploaded."* [1](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6332943?hl=en)

## 2. Uske baad ye 6 cheezein

1. **Earn** section me Shorts Monetization Module ka status human check karein (channel-level) [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en).
2. **AI use** toggle (Attributes) abhi bhi unconfirmed hai — human confirm karein [10](workspace://research/long_form_viral_idea_v11/LIVE_SHORT_DESCRIPTION_AUDIT_V11.md).
3. **Visibility** confirm karein; chahein to clearance tak **Unlisted / Private** rakhna ek ihtiyati option hai (aapka faisla, koi guarantee nahi) [4](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407?hl=en).
4. **Thumbnail** me astronaut ka chehra na ho [5](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
5. **Description** waisa hi rakhein — mat badlein, mat hataayein; credit + no-endorsement + disclosure wahin rehne dein [10](workspace://research/long_form_viral_idea_v11/LIVE_SHORT_DESCRIPTION_AUDIT_V11.md).
6. Video **delete** karne ka mashwara main nahi de raha; ye aapka faisla hai. Main koi bhi natija (safe / hataya jayega / strike) claim nahi karta.

## 3. Kyun itni jaldi? (5 forensic findings)

### F1 — Ads ON ka matlab rights ka izhaar

YouTube official: *"By turning on ads for videos on YouTube, you confirm that you have all the necessary rights to the visual and audio elements of those videos."* [2](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522?hl=en) Hamare committed ledger me `full_legal_review=false` aur likhit permission `false` hai [8](workspace://production/monarch-cam/caught_on_camera_commentary_v10/review/final/upload_metadata_v10.json). Aapki apni description bhi kehti hai *"no written permission, human legal review or legal opinion is claimed"* [8](workspace://production/monarch-cam/caught_on_camera_commentary_v10/review/final/upload_metadata_v10.json). Ye do baatein aapas me match nahi karti — isliye ye sabse pehle dekhne wali jagah hai.

### F2 — NASA ka commercial / identifiable-person gate seedha yahan lagta hai

NASA: *"If a NASA image, audio, video or media includes an identifiable person, using the media for commercial purposes may infringe that person's right of privacy or publicity, and permission should be obtained from the person."* aur *"Astronauts or employees who are currently employed by NASA cannot have their names, likenesses or other personality traits displayed ... on any commercial products, advertisements, promotional material or commercial product packaging."* [5](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) Is clip me identifiable astronauts / possible CSA personnel hain [8](workspace://production/monarch-cam/caught_on_camera_commentary_v10/review/final/upload_metadata_v10.json). NASA short-form social ko generally promotional kehta hai [7](https://www.nasa.gov/collaborating-with-nasa/strategic-partnerships/).

### F3 — Shorts ki monetization do level ki hoti hai

Shorts Feed ads **channel-level** revenue sharing hai: *"Only monetizing partners who've accepted the Shorts Monetization Module can earn ad revenue from Shorts"* [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en). Isliye 'channel YPP ON', 'is video ke ads ON' aur 'Shorts module accepted' teen alag baatein hain. Individual video ka monetization OFF kiya ja sakta hai [1](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6332943?hl=en), lekin **Shorts Feed pool se is ek video ko alag se kaise hataya jaye, iski guarantee mujhe official docs me nahi mili** — `Earn` section human check karein. Main yahan koi certainty claim nahi karta.

### F4 — Ads ON ≠ ads chal gaye ≠ paisa mila

YouTube: *"Choosing to turn on ads doesn't mean that ads will automatically open on a video. Before any ads show up, the video will go through a standard process including automated or human reviews"* [2](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522?hl=en). Aur Shorts module accept karne se **pehle** wale views revenue-sharing ke liye eligible nahi [3](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en). Isliye main yahan koi ad-run, revenue, strike ya takedown ka dawa nahi karta — na hi koi violation ka faisla.

### F5 — Description achhi thi, par wo clearance nahi deti

Credit + no-endorsement + synthetic VO disclosure + 'no legal opinion claimed' sab theek hain [10](workspace://research/long_form_viral_idea_v11/LIVE_SHORT_DESCRIPTION_AUDIT_V11.md). Lekin wo sab **risk kam** karte hain, **commercial-use ki ijazat** nahi dete. Pehle wala decision-card bhi yahi kehta tha: monetized/promotional = abhi nahi [9](workspace://research/long_form_viral_idea_v11/UPLOAD_SAFETY_V11.md).

## 5. Monetization dobara ON kab?

- NASA Office of Communications ko **written request / approval** for the intended commercial use (public contact: `nasa-merchandise-&-brand-team@mail.nasa.gov`, specific-video questions 202-358-0309) [5](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) [6](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/advertising-guidelines/).
- Identifiable astronauts / possible CSA personnel ki **apni likhit permission** — NASA unki taraf se nahi de sakta [5](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) [6](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/advertising-guidelines/).
- Ek **insaan ka legal review** jo is clip, script, thumbnail, monetization plan aur region (PK) ko dekhe [8](workspace://production/monarch-cam/caught_on_camera_commentary_v10/review/final/upload_metadata_v10.json).
- Tab jaakar monetization dobara ON karne ka faisla **aap** karein; main tab bhi 'guaranteed safe' nahi kahunga.
