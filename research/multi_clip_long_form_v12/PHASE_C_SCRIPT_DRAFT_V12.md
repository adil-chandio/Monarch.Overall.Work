### 2. `0:20-0:55` — The promise and the rule

*Budget 75 words · draft 85 words · `DRAFT_NOT_RECORDED_NOT_RENDERED`*

**Shots:** Presenter on a clean card. One sentence per line, no stock b-roll.

**VO:**

Here is the rule, stated once, plainly. A moment becomes rewatchable when it breaks an expectation you did not know you had, and the explanation arrives one beat later than your brain wanted it. Not the surprise alone. Not the explanation alone. The gap between them. Over the next ten minutes I will test that rule on eight completely different moments. If the rule is real, it should survive all eight. If it does not, I will say so. No outcome promises. Just the mechanism.

### 3. `0:55-1:40` — Why 'rewatched or shared' is a real signal

*Budget 110 words · draft 132 words · `DRAFT_NOT_RECORDED_NOT_RENDERED`*

**Shots:** Original metric card: Spikes / Intro / Dips, drawn by us. On-screen source note.

**VO:**

Quick note on where this idea comes from. YouTube's own help pages call those moments Spikes, points that get rewatched or shared. Intro is the share still watching after thirty seconds. Dips are where people skip or leave. Those reports belong to the channel owner. They need at least sixty seconds and a hundred views, and they take a day or two to appear. I do not have them for anyone else's channel, and I am not going to pretend I do. What I can use is public information: view counts, titles, durations, published dates, and the structure a creator chose to put in their own description. So this is a structural argument, not a claim about anyone's retention graph. That distinction matters for the rest of the video.

## 3. Edit decisions (kyun, sirf style nahi)

| Rule | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| J-cut / L-cut between clip and commentary | Keeps the explanation arriving one beat late - the thesis itself, not just a style choice. |
| One original diagram per clip, drawn by us | Makes the repair visible and keeps the video commentary-dominant, not a compilation. |
| Native audio retained; no music or SFX substitution | Slot 5's A/B comparison is only valid with the real audio. Also a rights requirement. |
| Three labelled replay passes (1x / 0.5x / 0.25x) on slot 3 | Real slow-motion winners show the reveal lives in a single frame. |
| No clip strung together with little narrative; no mostly non-verbal reaction | Official reused-content policy line [7](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en) |
| Source share <=80s / 600s (<=13.3%), each clip <=10s | Original commentary must dominate; permission alone is not enough [7](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en) |
| No ALL CAPS, no coercion, no reward CTA | Packaging and spam rules [6](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en) [14](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en) |
