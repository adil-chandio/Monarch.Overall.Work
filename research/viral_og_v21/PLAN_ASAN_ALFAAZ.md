## 3. Kaise banayenge — 8 step, har video ke liye

1. **Topic chuno** (mera list neeche hai). Ek sawal, ek line me.
2. **Research (2–3 ghante).** 3–5 asli papers ya books. Har number ka source note karo. Jo cheez confirm na ho, wo video se **hata do**.
3. **Script likho (3–4 ghante).** 1,600 words = ~9 minute. 6 chapters. Pehla 30 second = scene, lecture nahi.
4. **Voice record** (30 min). Apni awaaz behtar hai; AI voice ho to description me likhna zaroori.
5. **3D scene banao.** Ek hi cave set banao — baar baar wahi use karo, sirf roshni aur camera badlo. 44 shots ka plan pehle se ready hai (`SHOTLIST_EP01_V21.csv`).
6. **Edit.** Slow camera, koi fast cut nahi. Fire ki awaaz + ek low music layer.
7. **Thumbnail.** Base image + 3 word text. Box, arrow, chehra close-up — kuch nahi.
8. **Upload.** Title, description (chapters ke saath), 19 tags, pinned comment jisme "corrections welcome" likha ho.

**Pehli video ka poora kaam ready hai:** script, 44 shot list, thumbnail — teeno file me hain.

## 5. Kya nahi karna (ye galtiyan data me saaf dikhti hain)

- ❌ Popular title **copy** karna — 31 channels ne kiya, 41–197 views mile.
- ❌ 3 minute se chhoti video se shuru karna — pahunch zyada, ceiling kam.
- ❌ "AI se banaya" chhupana — 240 comments me ek bhi AI complaint nahi thi, yani log farq nahi karte, lekin sach bolna behtar.
- ❌ Emoji/hashtag title me dalna.
- ❌ Jhooti cheez bolna ("scientists ne discover kiya" jab paper nahi hai). Ek galat video = channel ka trust khatam.
- ❌ Ads/sponsor se shuru karna — comments me sab se zyada tareef "no ads, straight to the point" ki thi.
