# VERIFIED SOURCES — Episode 01 (cave bear)

**Status: 11 me se 11 claims researched. 3 galtiyan pakri gayin aur script me theek ki gayin.**
Ye file publish se pehle ka record hai. Har line ke saath source hai, aur jahan source ne script badal di, wo `CORRECTION` me likha hai.

## 1. Chauvet Skull Chamber — skull on a rock ✅ (magar ek ahem qualifier ke saath)

**Source:** Ministère de la Culture, `archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/chauvet/en/skull-chamber`; Quiles et al. 2016, *PNAS* 113(8): "A high-precision chronological model for the decorated Upper Paleolithic cave of Chauvet-Pont d'Arc".

**Jo source kehta hai:**
- Skull Chamber me ek cave bear skull **rocky block ke centre par rakha hua** hai ("set on a rocky block in the centre gives the chamber its name").
- Us chamber me **53 skulls** inventoried hue (poori cave me 190+).
- Skulls ki density baaki cave se kaafi zyada — is liye "intentional collection" ka hypothesis banta hai.
- Human occupation: **37,000–33,500** aur **31,000–28,000** saal pehle. Bears: **~33,000** tak.
- ⚠️ **Quiles 2016 saaf kehta hai:** "even if the first human occupation phase and the cave bear occupation phase fall within the same broad time interval, **this overlap does not mean that both were present in the cave at the same time**."

**`CORRECTION #1`** Purani script kehti thi "humans and cave bears were competing for the same caves… sometimes in the same layers" — Chauvet ke liye ye **ghalat** hai. Theek kiya: Chauvet me wo **ek doosre ke baad** aaye, saath nahi. "Same cave" ka claim sirf un sites par jahan evidence hai (Hohle Fels, Deszczowa, Rio Secco, Fumane) — aur wahan **butchering** ka evidence hai, sirf "saath rehna" nahi.

**`CORRECTION #2`** "Thirty-two thousand years ago someone put a skull on a rock" — Chauvet ke paintings 37–33.5k ya 31–28k ke hain; skull ki apni date fix nahi. Theek kiya: "somewhere between thirty-seven and twenty-eight thousand years ago" + "we can't date the gesture, only the room."

## 2. Cave bear size ✅ (magar "bigger than any bear alive" ghalat tha)

**Source:** standard morphometric summaries of *Ursus spelaeus* (male **350–600 kg**, female **225–250 kg**, some specimens up to ~1,000 kg); comparison: large Kodiak/polar bears reach similar or greater mass today.

**`CORRECTION #3`** Purani script kehti thi "bigger than any bear alive today". Ye **defensible nahi** hai — Kodiak aur polar bear aaj bhi 600 kg+ hote hain. Theek kiya: **"as heavy as the largest brown bears alive today — but built differently"**: steeper forehead, more massive skull, grinding teeth. Ye zyada sach hai aur **zyada interesting** bhi.

---

## 3. Diet — "vegetarian" ✅ magar nuance zaroori

**Sources:**
- Bocherens et al. — bulk collagen δ¹⁵N cave bear values herbivore-level (classic herbivory signal), multiple European sites.
- Naito et al. 2016, *J. Quaternary Science* (Goyet Cave, Belgium) — amino-acid δ¹⁵N: trophic position **1.9–2.1 = purely herbivorous**; brown bear 2.0–2.4.
- Peigné et al. 2009, *PNAS* — dental microwear, Goyet: **predormancy omnivory** (hibernation se pehle kuch animal matter).
- **Richards et al. 2008, *PNAS* 105(2):600–604** (Peştera cu Oase, Romania) — us population ke isotopes **omnivory/carnivory** dikhate hain.
- Münzel et al. 2014 — isotopes + tooth microwear, behavioural ecology.

**Nateeja:** "mostly plants, in most populations" — **sach hai**. "Strictly vegetarian, always" — **ghalat** hai.

**Script me kaise:** "The teeth say it. The chemistry of the bones says it. In most places, this was mostly a plant eater… **at least one population was eating meat.**" — ye line dono camps ko cover karti hai aur audience ko lagta hai ke hum imandaar hain.

## 4. Humans reached Europe ~45,000 BP ✅

**Source:** standard Aurignacian dispersal literature; Chauvet ke artists Aurignacian hain, jo ~40,000 saal pehle Europe pahunche. Safe phrasing: **"roughly forty-five thousand years ago"** → actually safest: **"around forty thousand years ago"**. Script me "forty-five" ko "forty" kar diya.

---

## 5. Bear bone accumulations in caves ✅

**Source:** cave bear fossils ki vast majority caves se aati hai, aur bohot se assemblages **hibernation ke doran maut** ko reflect karte hain (Pacher & Stuart 2009, "Extinction chronology and palaeobiology of the cave bear"). Chauvet me hi 190+ skulls, 200+ individual skeletons.

**Script line:** "Thousands of bear skeletons have come out of single cave systems. Not because bears were killed there. Because they went there to sleep, and some never woke up." ✅ accurate.

---

## 6. Alternating occupation / same caves ✅ (specific sites)

**Source:** Romandini et al. 2018 (Grotta Rio Secco & Grotta Fumane, Italy) — Neanderthals ne **1,700+ bear bones** wali assemblage me cave bear ko hunt aur butcher kiya; cut marks + carnivore tooth marks dono; **spring ambush** (hibernation ke baad, females kamzor) ka argument; caves ko phir lodging ke tor par use kiya.

**Script me add:** ye **naya, strong beat** hai — "they didn't fight bears in the open. They waited until spring."

---

## 7. Cut marks / marrow fractures ✅ (ab solid)

**Sources:**
- **Hohle Fels (Swabian Jura)** — cave bear **vertebra with an embedded flint projectile point**; cut aur impact marks.
- **Deszczowa Cave** — mandible, thoracic vertebra, metapodials par cut marks (skinning + dismemberment).
- **Pavlov I**, **Krakow Spadzista** — cut-marked tibia; cave bear fibula se awl; **cave bear incisor se pendant**.
- **Schöningen (Germany, ~320,000 BP)** — metatarsal + phalanx par "delicate butchering" cut marks → **bear skin exploitation**, Lower Palaeolithic.
- Ciota Ciara (Italy) — Neanderthal cut marks on *U. spelaeus*.

**`IMPORTANT`** Purani script kehti thi "we don't have a scene". Ye ab **ghalat** hai — Hohle Fels ki vertebra me **flint point dhansa hua** mila hai. Wo ek scene hai. Script me "we have no scene" hataa kar ye rakh diya: **"a vertebra with a stone point still stuck in it."**

## 8. Human bones with carnivore tooth marks ✅ magar rarity

**Source:** Romandini et al. 2018 (Rio Secco/Fumane) — bear aur human remains dono par carnivore modifications; hyena tooth marks on cave bear cub femora (Diedrich 2014).

**Script me:** "rare, genuinely rare — and rare is exactly what you'd expect". ✅ Qualitative rakha, koi fake number nahi.

## 9. Bear skull "cult" — Regourdou, Drachenloch, Drachenhöhle ✅ (dispute real hai)

**Sources:**
- **Drachenloch (Switzerland)**, Emil Bächler 1917–1923 — stone "chests" with bear skulls, muzzles facing the entrance; long bones threaded through cheekbones.
- **Regourdou (France)**, Bonifay 1950s–60s — Neanderthal burial + ~20 bear skulls in a pit under a ~1-ton slab.
- **Critiques:** **Chase & Dibble 1987**; **Gargett 1989** — natural processes (water flow, frost-heave, bears moving bones, rockfall) explain the arrangements.
- Modern summaries: "some critiques have themselves been contested; what is not disputed is that Neanderthals interacted with and arranged faunal remains."

**Script me:** dono taraf rakhe — "a bear cult" aur "a skull that fell". Ye **sahi** hai aur episode ka best beat hai.

## 10. Cave bear in cave art ✅

**Source:** Chauvet "Recess of the Bears" — **three cave bears painted in red**, central bear ~120 cm, wall relief ko shoulder ke tor par use kiya; cave bears unke **steep forehead** se pehchane jate hain. Chauvet me 420+ animal representations, aur predators (lions, rhinos, bears) un hunted animals se zyada hain.

**Script line:** "one of the first animals they painted, in red, in the dark, was the bear" ✅ accurate.

## 11. Extinction — do camps, dono strong ✅

**Sources:**
- **Pacher & Stuart 2009** — 24,000 ¹⁴C BP (~27,800 cal BP), Greenland Stadial 3 ke shuru me; "climatic cooling and inferred decreased vegetational productivity were probably responsible".
- **Baca et al. 2016** — extinction 26,100–24,300 cal BP; GS-3; "climate deterioration lowered vegetation productivity, on which the cave bear strongly depended as a strict herbivore".
- **MPI / Stiller et al. (2010)** — mtDNA: decline **50,000 saal pehle shuru** hua, aur "caused **more by human expansion** than by climate change".
- Later reviews: "The ultimate cause(s) of extinction are a matter of debate."

**`CORRECTION #4`** Purani script kehti thi "we were one of the reasons, not the reason" — ye **ek camp** ka mawqif hai, hakeekat nahi. Theek kiya: dono camps naam se, aur phir wo line: **"Two camps. Both have DNA and dates. Neither has the answer."**
