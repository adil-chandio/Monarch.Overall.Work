Back to the skull on the rock.
It isn't one skull. In more than one cave, researchers found cave bear skulls that look like they were put somewhere — set on ledges, placed in niches, sometimes with long bones crossed underneath. 
Out of that came one of the most exciting ideas in archaeology: a bear cult. Our first religion, and it was about an animal.
 Then the sceptics arrived. And they were right about a lot. A cave is a terrible place to keep a story. Bones roll. Water moves them. Ice pushes them. Later people move them again. A skull on a ledge might just be a skull that fell.
 So take away everything that could be an accident. What's left?
Something narrower, and stranger. Humans and bears walked into the same small number of dark rooms, over and over, for thousands of years. And when humans finally painted on walls, one of the first animals they painted — in red, in the dark — was the bear. 
Praying to it, or living in its leftovers — they could not stop thinking about it.
