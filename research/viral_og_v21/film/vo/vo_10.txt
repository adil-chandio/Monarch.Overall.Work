Think about what that person did. They walked into the dark, into a place that belonged to something bigger than them, that could fill the only door. And then they made a place for the animal they'd displaced. Not a trophy. A position. On a rock. In the dark.
 The first room our ancestors ever decorated was somebody else's bedroom. They moved into a bear's house, and then they put its head on a shelf.
 There are still bears in caves. Fewer. Smaller. In fewer places. And there are still people who walk into the dark with a light, look at something on a rock, and understand that this place was somebody's before it was ours.
 The skull is still there. It's just not a bear's any more.
