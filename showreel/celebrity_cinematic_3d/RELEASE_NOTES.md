A further art-directed, fictional 60-second 3D reconstruction of the celebrity
story. The ten narration takes remain, with animated skinned characters, a
remodeled bedroom and kitchen, street architecture and pavement, a 3D car and
foliage, revised seated poses, lighting, materials and cameras. No photographic
background plates are used.

Rendered in Blender Cycles at 720×1280, 24 fps, eight samples with denoising.
This is a stylized, low-sample animated preview, not a photorealistic or
production cinema master. Facial animation is not lip-synced; contacts and foot
planting are approximate, and captions use estimated rather than word-aligned
timing. The synthetic music and foley are retained. See ASSET_CREDITS.txt for
all sources and required attribution.
