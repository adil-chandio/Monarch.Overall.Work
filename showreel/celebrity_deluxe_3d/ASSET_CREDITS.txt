The above sounds are mixed, panned, faded and level-adjusted for this film.
The concrete/wood steps and camera shutter are repeated in sync with the
animation; no new spoken dialogue is represented as the characters' voices.
The files' exact sources, Git blob identifiers and SHA-256s are in assets.json.
