A further art-directed 60-second fictional celebrity reconstruction, rendered in 3D. The original ten narration takes are retained. This revision adds hand-following phone pickup, a hollow ceramic cup, closer palm contact, extra bystanders and set details, more intimate shot coverage, a night skyline and a new stereo mix using credited recordings for piano, crowd, traffic and foley.

Native Blender Cycles render: 1080×1920, 24 fps, 16 samples with denoising; H.264/AAC. This is a stylized preview, not a photorealistic or fully hand-animated master. Faces are not lip-synced; hands, prop interaction, foot planting, the vehicle cutaway and architectural detail remain approximate. Caption splits follow narration pauses, not word-level timing. Denoising may flicker in motion. Numerical checks and sampled frames do not substitute for continuous viewing and critical listening.
