A newly rendered, fictional 60-second 3D revision of the rebuilt preview. It keeps the ten-cue narration while upgrading to skinned, animated Rocketbox characters, Cycles lighting, textured furniture, revised cameras, and a modeled moving car door.

This is a 480×854, low-sample, game-like/stylized preview, **not** a photorealistic cinema master or a recovery of the lost original. Sets and car remain simple, contacts and facial motion are approximate, and captions are estimated rather than word-aligned. The character is a generic fictional avatar, not a real person's likeness.

The synthetic narration, synthesized music and foley are retained from the preceding reconstruction. Third-party licenses and credits are attached as `ASSET_CREDITS.txt`; the complete editable renderer and build notes are in the repository under `showreel/celebrity_premium_3d/`.
