What is real in this cut
------------------------

- six painted poses from one model sheet, each keyed off the green field and registered with its own rig
- a crop-and-resample camera on an overscanned plate, so the push-ins are camera moves and not zooms
- motivated light with a slow flicker, distance haze, shallow depth of field, contact shadow, grade and film grain
- every frame computed from its own timestamp

What is not
-----------

This is 13 seconds of held painted poses cut together, not a continuous performance.
The single-cel rig that preceded it could not lift an arm past roughly 30 degrees,
because a side view of that arm does not exist in the source drawing. A full-length
cut needs a larger painted pose library and separately painted face close-ups.

Colour: Rec.709, h264 high profile, yuv420p, faststart.
