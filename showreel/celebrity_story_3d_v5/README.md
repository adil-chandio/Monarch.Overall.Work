# Celebrity life, portrait edit

A fictional, third-person 3D celebrity-day short based on the previous v4 project. Reference inspected: the user-provided 45.84-second vertical school-story video (1080×1920, 29.97 fps). Forty-six one-second samples were visually inspected; its scene-by-scene subtitles, frequent close-ups, motivated insert shots and clear emotional story informed the portrait presentation. The reference was not incorporated in the deliverable and no listening pass of its audio was performed. It has far richer assets, performances and environments; this edition is not a match to its animation quality.

Changes: portrait 540×960 output and larger subject framing, short outlined captions with approximate text-proportional timing, a fresh continuous-flow narration assembled from seven trimmed phrases with 100 ms between the source clips, picture retimed to the narration, and additional camera angle changes. The voice is the selected synthetic narrator, without impersonating a real person. The music/effects remain procedural from v4, with public-event shutter accents retimed to match flash events. The first caption text and corresponding narration start near the beginning; final clip ends before 60 seconds. Speech intervals are determined by amplitude trimming and are not human-listened or forced-aligned. The clips were not time-stretched. Captions may lead or lag the exact spoken words.

Motion is still adapted Rocketbox animation: props follow wrist-relative transforms, not true hand-grip or collision simulation; walking can slide; a fan and hero perform approximate gestures. The room, crowd and acting are sparse and stylized. The render cannot be described as photoreal, flawless, reference-equivalent or fully physically accurate. See v4 README and asset manifest for source credits. Run `assemble.py` after 12 five-second shards are rendered with `--width 540 --fps 24 --samples 3`; `mux.py` adds the stereo synthetic score and voice cues. Note: `narration.wav` is an unused single-take alternative, not the delivered master.
