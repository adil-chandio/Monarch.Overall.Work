# monarch-thumbnail-craft

A thumbnail is not art direction. It is a 168×94 px argument, made in 0.4 seconds, to
someone who was not looking for you.

So we render three different **bets**, measure all three at feed size, and ship the winner.

```bash
python -m showreel.ctr            # renders + scores + writes public/showreel/ctr_report.json
```

---

## 1. The three archetypes (three different bets, not three skins)

| Archetype | Bet | When it wins |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Number slam** | A large, verifiable number *is* the promise | you have a real receipt: views, speed, price, frames |
| **B · Face + 3 words** | A face plus three words out-punches any graphic | the work is personal, the audience buys the person |
| **C · Contrast split** | Two states, one slash, one word | the idea is "them vs us", before/after, promises vs proof |

All three are rendered deterministically with Pillow from assets already in the repo
(Anton + IBM Plex Mono, the masked portrait, the studio palette). No stock, no AI images,
no network — so a thumbnail can be regenerated byte-identically in CI.

---

## 2. How they are scored

Five measurements, weighted, all explainable:

| Metric | Weight | Ideal | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mobile_survival` | 0.30 | ≥ 0.86 | downsample to 168×94, upsample back, correlate with the original. Detail that dies at feed size is detail that does not exist |
| `contrast` | 0.20 | std ≈ 0.45 luma | a low-contrast thumb disappears next to a bright one |
| `focal_gravity` | 0.20 | 0.55–0.85 of edge energy in the inner frame | the eye needs one place to land, and the frame edge must stay quiet |
| `palette_discipline` | 0.15 | hue entropy ≤ 0.55 | three colours read; nine colours look like a template |
| `accent_presence` | 0.15 | 2–22 % vivid pixels | enough accent to guide, not enough to shout |

Hard fails (not deductions):
- `mobile_survival < 0.70` → **does not survive the feed**
- bottom-right busy (`> 0.34` luma std) → **the duration overlay will collide**

Grades: A ≥ 85, B ≥ 72, C ≥ 58, D below.

Current studio suite: **B 97.3 · C 94.6 · A 91.7** (all grade A, no hard fails).

## 3. Layout laws learned the hard way

1. **Place type by ink bounds, not font metrics.** `textbbox((0,0), …)` reports Anton's
   em box, which is far taller than the caps. Every collision in the first three draft
   renders came from trusting it. Render the number to a mask, take `getbbox()`, position
   everything from the real glyph extents.
2. **Fit the type to the free column.** Size search downward until the string fits inside
   `portrait_left - margin`. Never let a headline run under a face unless the overlap is
   deliberate and high-contrast.
3. **A rule above a number reads as a strike-through.** Accent rules go *below* the ink,
   and clear of descenders (the comma in `583,290` descends 30 px further than the digits).
4. **Paint the portrait last** so frame rules break at the subject instead of slicing
   across it. That single ordering change is what makes A look editorial instead of broken.
5. **Keep the bottom-right 20 × 14 % clear.** YouTube prints the duration there.

---

## 4. The rules of honesty (non-negotiable)

- The number on the thumbnail **must exist** in the repo, a studio capture, or the client
  portal. A thumbnail that out-claims the video raises CTR and destroys retention — the
  two cancel, and retention is the one the algorithm reads.
- No clickbait constructions. `showreel/ctr/title.py` hard-fails on
  "you won't believe", "gone wrong", "exposed", fake urgency.
- Faces are used as *identity*, never as a shocked-expression prop.

---

## 5. Workflow

1. `python -m showreel.ctr` → three JPEGs in `public/showreel/thumbs/` plus a
   `__feed.jpg` proof for each.
2. Read the report: `score`, `grade`, `hard_fails`, `raw.mobile_survival`.
3. Open the `__feed.jpg` files at 100 %. If you cannot read the idea there, delete it.
4. Pick the top scorer that matches the **content's** bet. Score does not override fit:
   a face-words thumb on a faceless channel is a lie about the product.
5. Ship one. Keep the runner-up for the second-week rotation.
